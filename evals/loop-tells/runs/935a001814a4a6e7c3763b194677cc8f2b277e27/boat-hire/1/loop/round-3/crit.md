**First read.** On desktop the eye lands on the serif headline "Hire a narrowboat for a week on the canal". It moves down to the lead, then across to the white "Find a boat" card and its dark green Search button. The page is calm and tidy, but it reads as a booking form with a headline. Nothing on it makes the visitor want to be on the water. On the phone and tablet the card stacks under the lead, and the tablet column now runs out to the same right edge as the nav. On widescreen the page is the desktop layout centred, with a wide empty band below it.

**What it's for.** It gets a visitor to pick a base, dates and a boat size and search for a narrowboat to hire (from the work order's crit).

**Verdict.** This round closed only the tablet margin finding. The page does its job a little more neatly, but the biggest thing in the way has not changed: there is still no picture of a boat or the canal, so nothing sells the trip.

**Finding status**
- no-picture-of-boat: open. There is still no photo of a boat, a lock or a towpath at any of the four viewports, and the hero is text only.
- h1-weight-and-color: open. The measured font_weight of h1 is still 400 at desktop, phone, tablet and widescreen, and the color is still #26302f at 12.75:1.
- start-date-text-field: open. The Start date field still shows "Sat 14 June" as plain text, with no year and no picker cue, at every viewport.
- nav-no-current-page: open. No nav item has an underline, a heavier weight or any other mark at any viewport.
- tablet-column-nav-edge: closed. On the tablet the h1, the lead, the Find a boat card and the routes list now end at x 704, which is the right edge of "Your booking" (box 604 + 100). Before, they ended at x 664.

**New problems.** None. The wider tablet column only rewraps the headline to "…a week on / the canal" and leaves the lead at about 63 characters a line.

**What would move it most**
1. No picture of a boat or the canal (no-picture-of-boat). This is in the hero and across the whole page at every viewport. The visitor sees text and a form, and nothing shows them the boat, the locks or the towpath they would be paying for. A holiday page sells the experience before the booking, so the page reads as a utility. Put real photos of the actual boats or routes into the hero, for example beside or behind the headline. On desktop and widescreen the empty band below the routes is also free. The maker must supply the photos. Do not use stock images. Taste. Viewports: desktop, phone, tablet, widescreen.

**Smaller things.**
- The Start date field (start-date-text-field) shows "Sat 14 June" with no year and no picker. Use a date input that shows the year. Viewports: desktop, phone, tablet, widescreen.
- The headline (h1-weight-and-color) is weight 400, the same as the lead. A semibold weight of the serif would give it the extra step. Viewports: desktop, phone, tablet, widescreen.
- The nav (nav-no-current-page) has no current-page cue. Give the active item one cue, such as an underline or a heavier weight. Viewports: desktop, phone, tablet, widescreen.

**What's working.**
- The tablet column, the card and the routes list now share one right margin with the nav, so the page sits on a single frame.
- The routes list sets each name against its nights and locks, with light rules between rows. It is easy to scan, and its numbers sit on one right edge.
- The Search button is the only strong color block on the page, so the next step is clear at every viewport.
