**First read.** The eye lands on the serif headline, drops to the intro, then crosses to the white "Find a boat" card and its dark green Search button. It reads as a quiet, honest booking page for a small boatyard: calm and tidy, but it is all text and form fields. On the phone and tablet the page is one column, and the form follows straight after the intro with nothing between them. On the tablet the form now runs the full width of the header.

**What it's for.** It gets a visitor to search for a narrowboat hire direct from the boatyard (taken from the work order's crit).

**Verdict.** The fixes made the page simpler. The extra link is gone, and the tablet column now lines up with the nav. But the page still asks the visitor to take "no fee on top of the hire" on trust, and it still shows no boat and no water. Both of those need material from the boatyard.

**New problems.** None. The only changed elements are the wider column and form card on the tablet and the removed link. Neither one is a template tell, and nothing new was added.

**What would move it most**
1. The no-fee claim has no number behind it. Where: the intro under the headline, at every viewport. The visitor reads "no fee on top of the hire" but has no price to judge it against, so the page's main selling point stays unproven. Fix: show a fact that proves the claim near the intro or the form, such as a from-price per week for a boat at this base. Only the boatyard can supply that figure. Defect. Viewports: desktop, phone, tablet, widescreen.
2. A holiday page with no picture of the holiday. Where: the hero, at every viewport. People hire a narrowboat for the boat and the water, and the page shows neither, so it sells a form instead of a trip. Fix: add a real photo of the boatyard's own boats or of a listed route, not a stock picture. The boatyard must supply it. Taste. Viewports: desktop, phone, tablet, widescreen.

**Smaller things.**
- The start-date field has no date-picker cue, so the visitor cannot tell it wants a picked date (Nielsen heuristic 5). Fix: use a date input or add a calendar icon to the field. Viewports: desktop, phone, tablet, widescreen.
- On the tablet the form fields are now 594 px wide, which is very long for a value like "7 nights". Fix: put Nights and Berths side by side on one row, or cap the width of the form card. Viewports: tablet.

**What's working.**
- The page now has one clear action, Search. On the phone the visitor reaches the form right after the intro, with no competing link.
- On the tablet the logo, headline, form and route list now share one left edge and one right edge with the nav, so the column reads as one block.
- The route list ("7 nights, 38 locks") gives real, useful detail in a quiet two-column layout. Keep it.
