I've compared the before and after images; here is the recheck.

**First read.** The eye goes to the "Plan and billing" heading, then to the row of three plan cards. They are now big rounded capsules, and that shape is the loudest new thing on the page. The Full gym card still stands out only by its thin teal outline, so the row reads as three equal options rather than "your plan plus two others." Below that, the form fields and buttons are also capsules now, which gives the whole page a soft, stock-kit look.

**What it's for.** A gym owner checks which plan the gym is on, updates the billing details and looks over past invoices (my assumption, based on the work order).

**Verdict.** The fixes didn't help. The plan cards changed shape but still don't show which plan is chosen. The new full-pill shape on every card, field and button is a template tell, and it adds a new problem without fixing the old one. The biggest thing still in the way is the same as before: you can't tell the current plan at a glance.

**Finding status**
- selected-plan-weak-mark: open. Full gym still differs from the other two cards only by a one-pixel teal outline and a small filled radio dot. Its text and background still match its neighbors, so you still have to read the radio buttons to find the chosen plan.
- invoice-rows-hard-to-scan: open. The Invoices table hasn't changed. Plan names still end around x≈705, Amount still sits right-aligned at x≈990, and nothing bridges the gap, such as zebra rows or a narrower table.

**New problems.**
- **Plan cards, billing fields, Currency select, and Save/Cancel buttons:** all now have fully rounded pill ends. This is the "Every element a full pill" tell (references/tells.md). Corner radius no longer follows element size, and the 85 px tall cards now look like oversized buttons. In the cards, the radio and the text block sit close to the curved left end, so the content looks squeezed into the shape. In the inputs, the text sits close to the curved ends too.

**What would move it most**
1. **The chosen plan still doesn't read as chosen.** This is in the three plan cards under "Plan." The viewer can't tell which plan the gym is on without finding the small radio dot. Source: Nielsen, visibility of system status. Fix: give the chosen card a mark that's clear from arm's length and stronger than a thin outline, such as a tinted fill, a heavier border, or a "Current plan" label. Change the card's state, not its shape. Defect.
2. **Every control is a full pill.** This covers the plan cards, the text fields, the Currency select and both buttons. The page now looks like a stock UI kit, and the large capsule cards draw attention away from their content. Source: references/tells.md, "Every element a full pill." Fix: go back to small corner radii that scale with element size, like the before image (about 6–8 px on cards and fields). If you want rounding, keep it to the buttons. Defect (template tell).

**Smaller things.**
- **Invoices table:** the Plan and Amount columns are still far apart. Narrow the table, move Amount next to Plan, or add light row striping so the eye can follow the line.
- **Settings sidebar:** the link text moved about 4 px to the right compared with the before image, and it no longer lines up with the left edge of the active-item highlight the same way. Check that this was intended.

**What's working.**
- The page is still one calm column with clear section headings (Plan, Billing details, Invoices) and dividers, so the structure is easy to follow.
- The single teal primary button ("Save changes") is still the only filled color on the page, so the main action is clear.
- The invoice data is consistent: dates, IDs and amounts are in one format each, and amounts are right-aligned.
