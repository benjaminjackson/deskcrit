**What would move it most**
1. **The weekday chart uses rainbow, pill-shaped bars with no highlight.** This is in the Deliveries by weekday panel. The viewer meets six competing colors and can't tell what is being emphasized, and the panel pulls the eye away from the KPIs and the trend chart. Source: references/tells.md "Rainbow, glowing, or pill-shaped chart bars"; data visualization "Limit categorical colors in a chart".
