**What would move it most**
1. **Weekday chart values** (Bottles by weekday, middle right card): the viewer can see that Saturday is highest but can't read how far behind the other six days are. Put a small number on every bar, in the same style as "148", or add a light value axis like the visitors chart has (Tufte, *The Visual Display of Quantitative Information*). Convention.
2. **Direction on the comparison lines** (under each headline figure in the top row): the October comparison is what gives each figure meaning, but it is still the palest, smallest text on the page and doesn't say whether the number went up or down. Show the change and its direction, for example as a signed difference or percentage from the figures already on the card, and darken the text a step. Taste.
3. **Inconsistent number formatting** (Revenue headline and the Revenue and Bottles columns of the table): the mixed precision and the "09" make the figures look unreliable on a page whose whole job is figures. Pick one precision per kind of figure (whole dollars for totals, as before), apply it everywhere, and drop the leading zero. Defect.

- Best-selling wines table: put the columns back so Bottles sits closer to Revenue, as in the before image, or space the four columns evenly.
