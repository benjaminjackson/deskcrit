**What it's for.** It lets a studio owner check and change their plan, keep billing details current, and look over past invoices (assumed from the work order and the page).

**Verdict.** The fixes did not help the page do its job. The section headings Plan, Billing details and Invoices are the same size and weight as before. The only visible change is three new badges, which add noise and do nothing for skimming. The biggest thing still in the way is the weak step between the headings and the body text.

**Finding status**
- section-headings-barely-stand-out: open. Plan, Billing details and Invoices look the same as in the before image, only a little larger than the field labels such as "Billing email" (judged by eye; there is no facts file). The three blocks still can't be found by skimming.

**New problems.**
- Badges in three new places: Beta next to Reports in the top bar, New next to Consent forms in the side menu, and PRO on the Multi-studio plan card. Together with the New already on Integrations, the screen now has four badges. That breaks the template tell "PRO, New, and Beta badges everywhere" (references/tells.md: at most one quiet text badge per screen). The badges also push Settings to the right in the top bar.
- The PRO, Beta and New labels make claims the piece did not make before: that Multi-studio is a separate "pro" tier, that Reports is in beta, and that Consent forms is new. Only the maker knows if these are true. On the plan cards, PRO is also confusing, since all three cards are already price tiers.

**What would move it most**
1. Section headings barely stand out from the body text: these are Plan, Billing details and Invoices above their blocks. Readers still have to go line by line, because the headings barely differ from the labels and values under them, so the rules between the blocks do the separating on their own (Bringhurst, *The Elements of Typographic Style*, on heading hierarchy). Make a clear step in size or weight. For example, set the headings visibly larger and in a heavier weight than the field labels, and add a little more space above each heading than below it. **Defect.**
2. Badges on four elements: the top bar, the side menu, and the Multi-studio card. They pull the eye to the edges and the plan cards, away from the content, and they read as template decoration (references/tells.md, "PRO, New, and Beta badges everywhere"). Remove the badges that were added. If one badge is really needed for status, keep only that one. Keep PRO off the plan cards entirely, because the name and price already set the tier apart. **Convention.**

**Smaller things.** None.

**What's working.**
- The selected Studio card is shown with a teal border and a filled radio button, so the current plan is clear right away without shouting.
- The invoice table is calm and easy to read: the amounts are right-aligned, the dates use one format, and the rules are thin.
- The single teal primary button next to a quiet Cancel gives the form one clear action.
