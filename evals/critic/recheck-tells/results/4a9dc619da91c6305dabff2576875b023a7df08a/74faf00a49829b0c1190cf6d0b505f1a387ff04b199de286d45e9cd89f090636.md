**What would move it most**
1. **The line chart is read through the legend, not the lines.** Location: Shop visitors card, middle row on the left. To learn which line is which month, the viewer has to look up to the legend in the top-right corner and back down. The grey October line also nearly blends into the grid lines. Direct labelling keeps the label next to the data (Tufte, *The Visual Display of Quantitative Information*). Fix: put "November" and "October" at the right end of each line, drop the corner legend, and make the October line darker or thicker than the grid lines. *Convention.*
2. **Sidebar items no longer line up.** Location: left navigation, Orders through Settings. The viewer sees a ragged column of icons and labels in what should be the steadiest part of the page, which makes the whole page look less finished. Fix: put the icons and labels of every nav item back on the same x positions, matching the Overview row (labels at x=60). *Defect.*

**Smaller things.**
- Best-selling produce table, Sold and Revenue columns: there is still a wide gap between the names and the numbers. Move the number columns closer to Grower, or add a light hover/zebra row guide so each number is easy to trace to its item.

**What's working.**
- In Orders by weekday, the single blue Saturday bar with its "148" label gives one clear focal point and answers "when are we busiest" at a glance.
- Each figure in the top band has a small "… in October" line under it, so the month-over-month comparison is there without adding another chart.
- The restrained palette (one blue, otherwise greys) keeps the page calm and makes the blue elements carry the meaning.
