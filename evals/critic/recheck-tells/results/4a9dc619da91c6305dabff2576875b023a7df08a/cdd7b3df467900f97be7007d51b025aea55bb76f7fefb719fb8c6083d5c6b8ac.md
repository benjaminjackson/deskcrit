**First read.** The eye lands on the "Plan and billing" heading, then on the row of three pill-shaped plan cards, then down to the teal "Save changes" button. It reads as a tidy settings page for a climbing gym's billing. It feels softer than before, but generic: the plan cards, text fields, the Currency select, both buttons and the selected sidebar item are all now fully rounded pills, so everything has the same shape and nothing stands out. The three plan cards still look nearly identical.

**What it's for.** It lets a gym owner see which plan they are on, update billing details, and check past invoices (my assumption from the page and the work order).

**Verdict.** The fixes did not help the page do its job. The chosen plan is still marked only by a thin teal outline and a radio dot. The new step of rounding every element into a pill added a template tell without making the choice clearer.

**Finding status**
- selected-plan-weak-mark: open. The Full gym card still differs from the other two only by a thin teal outline and a filled radio dot. Its background, text and weight match its neighbours, so you still can't pick out the chosen plan without reading the radio buttons. The card's new pill shape is a template tell (see New problems).
- invoice-rows-hard-to-scan: open. The Invoices table is unchanged. Plan still ends near x≈705 and Amount sits at x≈990, with the same wide empty gap and no row aid.

**New problems.**
- The plan cards, the Billing email, Invoice address and Currency fields, the Save changes and Cancel buttons, and the selected "Plan and billing" sidebar item are all now drawn as full pills. This is the template tell "Every element a full pill" (references/tells.md). The three-line plan cards get the same pill ends as a one-line button, so the radius no longer follows the size of each element. The page now looks stock rather than made for this product, and the shape gives the chosen plan no extra signal because every card has it.

**What would move it most**
1. **Chosen plan still not obvious** (defect). In the three plan cards under "Plan", the viewer still has to find a small radio dot to know the gym is on Full gym. Source: Nielsen, visibility of system status. Direction: give the chosen card a mark the other two lack, such as a tinted teal background, a heavier border, or a "Current plan" label. It should be the first thing the eye finds in that row.
2. **Every element a full pill** (convention, template tell). This affects cards, fields, the select, buttons and the sidebar selection. The viewer meets a generic, rounded-everything look in which no shape means anything. Source: references/tells.md "Every element a full pill". Direction: undo the blanket pill radius. Size the radius to the element, with a modest radius on cards and fields and a smaller one on buttons, and keep the earlier crisp corners unless the brand calls for something else.

**Smaller things.**
- Invoices table, Plan to Amount gap: move Amount next to Plan, or add zebra rows or a hover line so the eye can follow the row across the gap.

**What's working.**
- The page structure is clear: a sidebar for settings sections, then three plainly separated groups (Plan, Billing details, Invoices) with clear headings and dividers.
- There is a single filled teal primary action ("Save changes") next to a quiet secondary "Cancel", so the main action is easy to spot.
