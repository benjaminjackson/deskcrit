**What it's for.** Letting a studio owner check and change their plan, keep billing details current, and look up past invoices. This is my assumption from the page, because the crit file wasn't available to me.

**Verdict.** The fixes didn't help the page do its job. The section headings look the same as before, and the only visible changes are three new badges, which add noise. The biggest thing still in the way is the weak heading step, which makes the three sections hard to find by skimming.

**Finding status**
- section-headings-barely-stand-out: **open.** "Plan", "Billing details" and "Invoices" look the same size and weight as in the before image. They are still only a little larger than the field labels and table headers, so they don't pass the "found at a glance" check. Judged by eye; there was no facts file.

**New problems.**
- **Badges added across the screen (template tell: "PRO, New, and Beta badges everywhere").** The page now has a "Beta" badge after Reports in the top nav, a "New" badge after Consent forms in the side nav, and a "PRO" badge after Multi-studio in the plan cards. Counting the "New" on Integrations that was already there, one screen now carries four badges, where the rule allows at most one quiet text badge. None of the new ones relate to the finding.
  - "PRO" is the worst of them. It labels one of three plans when every card already states its price, so it reads as an upsell tag rather than information.
  - The "Beta" badge also pushes "Settings" to the right in the top nav.

**What would move it most**
1. **The section headings still barely stand out** (Plan, Billing details, Invoices, each above its own block). The viewer has to read line by line to see where one block ends and the next begins. The headings are hardly bigger or darker than "Billing email", "Currency" or the "Date" column header. Bringhurst, *The Elements of Typographic Style*, on hierarchy of headings: each level needs a visible step. The fix is to make the step clear, using a noticeably larger size, a heavier weight, or both, and to keep the field labels at body size. **Defect.**
2. **Four badges on one screen** (top nav, side nav, Multi-studio plan card). They give the eye four small competing targets and make the page look templated (references/tells.md, "PRO, New, and Beta badges everywhere", unslop). The fix is to remove the "Beta", "New" (Consent forms) and "PRO" badges that were added, so the page is back to at most one quiet badge. Drop "PRO" first: the price already sets the plans apart. **Defect** (template tell).

**What's working.**
- The selected plan is shown with both a filled radio and an outlined card, so the current choice is clear without any extra labeling.
- The invoices table is quiet and easy to scan: amounts are right-aligned, the rules are light, and there's no decoration.
- There is a single primary action, "Save changes", in the accent green, with "Cancel" kept secondary. It's clear what to do once you've made changes.
