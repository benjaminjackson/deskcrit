It reads as a calm, ordinary settings page for a community garden. The new emoji in the count badges catch the eye before the labels beside them do: 👥 38, 🌱 12, 🔔 3 in the left menu and 🧾 12 next to "Invoices". They add a cute note that the rest of the page doesn't have.

**What it's for.** My assumption: the person who manages the garden checks and changes its plan and billing details here, and looks over past invoices.

**Verdict.** The fixes didn't help. The only change between the two images is emoji added to the four count badges. Neither finding was touched, and the change itself is a template tell. The biggest thing still in the way is the wide gap between Plan and Amount in the invoice table.

**Finding status**
- invoice-columns-far-apart: **open.** The table looks the same as before. Plan still sits at about x=640 and Amount ends at about x=980, with about 250px of empty space between them, no row shading, and only thin rules between rows.
- current-section-not-marked-twice: **open.** The left menu still has no heading, and nothing ties it to the "Settings" item in the top bar. The emoji in its badges don't help with that.

**New problems.**
- Left menu badges and the "Invoices" count: emoji were put inside the count pills (👥 38, 🌱 12, 🔔 3, 🧾 12). This is the template tell "Emoji inside pills and counts" (references/tells.md, unslop): a count should be a plain number. The emoji also make the pills different widths, so their left edges no longer line up in the menu. The emoji don't come from any icon set used elsewhere on the page.

**What would move it most**
1. **The invoice table's Plan and Amount columns are far apart.** In the Invoices table, the eye has to cross about 250px of empty space to get from a plan name to its amount, and it can slip to the next row on the way. By the law of proximity (Wertheimer), things that belong together should sit together. Fix: narrow the table, or move Amount next to Plan. Light row shading or a hover row would also help. *Convention.*
2. **Emoji in the count badges.** In the left menu and next to "Invoices", the emoji turn plain counts into decoration and make the page look like a stock template. They add no information. Fix: go back to plain number badges. *Convention (template tell).*

**Smaller things.**
- Left menu: add a small "Settings" heading above "Garden details" so it's clear the menu belongs to the Settings item in the top bar. (Still open from the work order; taste.)

**What's working.**
- The plan cards: the selected Garden card stands out through its teal border and filled radio button, and its price sits right under its description. Picking a plan is quick.
- The form layout: Billing email and Currency share a row, Invoice address spans the full width, and the single teal primary button keeps the action clear.
