**What would move it most**
1. **Centered hero stack (new problem):** the viewer gets a generic, centered landing page, and has to scroll to see the plan and its pricing. Before, the plan was in view next to the headline. Fix: go back to the two-column hero, with copy on the left and the plan beside it, so the plan and the button are both visible on first view. Sources: `references/tells.md` "Hero stacked on one centered axis"; `references/layout.md` "No central axis by default". *Convention.*

3. **Feature notes out of view (feature-row-reads-as-afterthought, worse):** visitor passes, charging bays and the warden's plan get no presence on the first screen. Fix: once the two-column hero is back, give the notes a clear place under it, for example by tightening the gap above the rule so they sit in the first view as the next step in reading. *Taste.*
