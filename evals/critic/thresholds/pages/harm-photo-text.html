Wake up above the river

Twelve log cabins above the river, each with a porch facing east. Breakfast is served from six, in time to see the sun reach the far wall.

Rooms

Cabins sleep two to four. Every one has a wood stove.

Trails

Three marked walks start at the lodge door.

Getting here

The shuttle meets the morning train at the valley station.
