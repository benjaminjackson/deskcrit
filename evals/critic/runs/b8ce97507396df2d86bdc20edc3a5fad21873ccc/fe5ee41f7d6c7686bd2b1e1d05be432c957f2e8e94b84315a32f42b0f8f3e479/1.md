**First read.** The eye lands on "Tell us about your project", moves right to the white form card and goes down the three fields. At the bottom it finds nothing to click. "Send message" looks like a leftover caption, not a button. The page is calm, plain and templated, and it reads as a small design studio's contact page.

**What it's for.** You told me nothing about it, so I've assumed this is a contact page where possible clients send a project enquiry to the studio.

**Verdict.** It doesn't do its job yet. The layout, labels and copy are sound, but the one action the page exists for has no button shape and no colour. Someone who has just written their message has to guess how to send it.

**What would move it most**
1. **"Send message" doesn't look like a button.** It sits at the bottom of the card, around 541–653 × 516–536. It's plain near-black text at body size, with no fill, border, underline or colour. It looks less weighted than the bold field labels above it and less clickable than the blue underlined nav and footer links, which lead away from the form. A visitor who has filled in all three fields reaches the end with nowhere obvious to click. That's the moment the studio either gets the enquiry or loses it. The page's only accent colour goes on the navigation and never reaches the main action. Material Design and Apple HIG ("Buttons") both reserve the strongest treatment for the primary action. The direction: make it a solid button in the link blue (or a darker shade) with white text, the strongest-looking control in the card, and at least 24 px tall (WCAG 2.5.8). As drawn, only the text line looks like a target. If this is a button whose styles failed to load, check that in a live browser. The still shows no sign of it. *Defect.*

**Smaller things.**
- **Right edge of the layout:** the header spans 56 to 1224, but the form card stops at about x=1068. That leaves an empty strip about 156 px wide inside the right margin, under the nav, and the 385 / 69 / 559 split of the body follows no clear proportion. On a studio's own page this looks unfinished. Put the intro and card on the header's columns (for example 5 + 7 of 12), or narrow the header to match. *Convention (Müller-Brockmann) for the grid; taste for the proportion.*
- **Headline line break:** it breaks as "Tell us about your / project", which strands the key noun on a short second line. Break it as "Tell us about / your project", or widen the box. *Taste.*
- **Field borders:** the light blue-grey borders on white look to be under 3:1. Measure them, and darken them one step if they fall short (WCAG 1.4.11).
- **Current page in the nav:** "Contact" at top right is styled exactly like the other three links. Mark the current page with one cue, such as weight or a bar (Nielsen heuristic 1; NN/g menu-design checklist).
- **Required fields:** no field is marked required or optional. Asterisk the required ones, or add "(optional)" to any that aren't (NN/g, Budiu).

**What's working.**
- **The intro copy:** "We read every message and reply within two working days" is a concrete promise people can hold the studio to. "Include a budget range and a rough timeline" tells them what to write, which makes the empty Message box less daunting. Keep both.
- **The form structure:** each field has a short bold label sitting tight above it (about 8 px) and well apart from the next field (about 31 px). No placeholder stands in for a label, and the labels and fields share an exact left edge at x=541.
- **The palette:** it's restrained, with one neutral range, one blue, links underlined as well as coloured, and one left margin at x=56 carrying the logo, headline and footer. That keeps the page calm. Once the blue is also on the button, it will point straight at the action.
