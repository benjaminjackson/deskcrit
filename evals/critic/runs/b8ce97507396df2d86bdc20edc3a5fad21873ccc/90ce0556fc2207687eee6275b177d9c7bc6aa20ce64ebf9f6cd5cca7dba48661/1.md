An adult who thinks "yes, Tuesdays could work" has no next step and leaves. A page should make it obvious within seconds what it is and what you can do there (Krug, *Don't Make Me Think*). The fix is one specific ask placed where the eye ends up, directly under the subline: for example "Book a trial class, Tuesday 7pm, [pool name]", with the price next to it. Naming a single first class lets the viewer try it at low risk instead of taking the promise on trust. Defect.
