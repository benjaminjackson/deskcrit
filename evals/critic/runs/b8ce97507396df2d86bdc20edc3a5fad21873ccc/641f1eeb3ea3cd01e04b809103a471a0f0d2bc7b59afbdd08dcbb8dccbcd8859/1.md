**What would move it most**
1. **The times passengers act on are vague.** Paragraph 1 says the ferry runs "between early morning and late evening". Paragraph 3 says winter boats run "each hour" and the last crossing "leaves the quay before dusk". These are the times a passenger holds the service to, and each can be read more than one way. "Before dusk" could mean 15:30 or 17:00 depending on the date. "Each hour" doesn't say on the hour or at a set minute past. A passenger who guesses wrong misses the last boat and is stuck on the far bank. The page then sends them to printed timetables "in the shelter at each landing", which they can only read once they're already there. Fix: give the first and last departure times for each season, or link to the timetable from this page. *Defect.*
2. **Nothing helps a reader scan for one topic.** The three paragraphs cover three separate topics: the timetable (y≈150–260), bicycles (y≈305–390), and winter (y≈430–540). All three are set as identical blocks with no subheads and no emphasis. Someone who only wants "can I bring my bike?" or "when's the last winter boat?" has to read everything. The answers ("every forty minutes", "no extra charge", "one boat each hour") sit mid-sentence. Fix: add short subheads or bold lead-ins (Timetable / Bicycles / Winter) so each paragraph can be found at a glance. *Taste*: it matters because this page gets consulted for one answer, not read through.
