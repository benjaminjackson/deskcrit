**What would move it most**

That is well above the 45 to 75 range Bringhurst gives as comfortable (*Elements of Typographic Style*, 2.1.2). On long lines the eye has further to travel back to the start of the next one, so readers lose their place. The paragraphs here are only one or two lines, so the cost is small now, but it will grow as soon as a section gets a real paragraph. Narrow the text column to about 620 to 680 px at this size, so lines land near 66 characters. The headings can keep the full width. *Convention.*
