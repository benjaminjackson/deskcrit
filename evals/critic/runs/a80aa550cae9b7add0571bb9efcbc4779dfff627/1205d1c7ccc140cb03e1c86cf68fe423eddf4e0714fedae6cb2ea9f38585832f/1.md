**Smaller things.**
- **Line break in the quote, line 2:** "the first ferry / leaves" splits the subject from its verb, so the reader pauses on "ferry" and then has to pick the sentence back up. Try breaking before "the first ferry" or after "leaves," if the measure allows.
- **Type scale at the small end:** I count three small sizes that are only a pixel or two apart: the kicker (about 15 px), the context line (about 16 px) and the name (about 18 px). Weight and colour already separate them. Set all three at one size and let bold versus grey do the work (Refactoring UI: bolder, not bigger).
