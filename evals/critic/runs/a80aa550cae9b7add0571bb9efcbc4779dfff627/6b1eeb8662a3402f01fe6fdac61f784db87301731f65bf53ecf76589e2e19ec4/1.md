**What would move it most**
1. **The visiting details are hidden in the prose, and the ones that matter most are missing.** This is a defect if the job is getting visitors.
   - **Where:** paragraphs 2 and 3, right column.
   - **What the reader experiences:** someone deciding whether to go has to read all three paragraphs to learn that the tower opens "on summer weekends" and that tickets are sold "at the cottage by the car park". They still don't know which months count as summer, what hours, what it costs, or where Gull Point is. "Summer weekends" can be read as June to August or as any warm weekend, and a reader who gets it wrong turns up to a locked door. Opening times and prices are what people hold you to, so they shouldn't be vague.
   - **Fix:** keep the story as it is, since it is the best thing here. Below it, add a small, scannable block (Krug: let people scan, not read): days and months open, hours, ticket price, and where to park. End with one specific action, such as a "Plan your visit" link or the address with a map link.
   - **Weight:** keep this block below the headline in weight, so it supports the story rather than taking it over.
