**What would move it most**
1. **The captions sit almost as close to the next picture as to their own.** In the right column, "Herb bed / Thyme, sage and chives." starts about 9 px below its picture and ends about 14 px above the Pond edge picture. The row gap between captions and the bottom row is the same. The viewer has to work out which picture each caption belongs to, and the whole grid reads as one solid block instead of six separate items. A caption should sit clearly nearer its own picture than the next one (proximity: Lupton, *Thinking with Type*; Refactoring UI). **Fix:** keep the picture-to-caption gap tight and roughly double or triple the gap between rows. Widening the column gutter to match would help too, since 14 px also looks cramped at this width. *Defect.*
2. **The hero picture's bottom edge lines up with nothing.** The Rose arch picture stops at about y=437, the Pond edge picture next to it stops at about y=474, and the two captions don't share a baseline either. Only the bottoms of the two caption blocks roughly meet. So the one seam that should look most deliberate looks slightly off, the kind of near-miss that reads as a mistake (Müller-Brockmann, *Grid Systems*: pictures take their top and bottom from the grid lines). **Fix:** make the hero picture end on the same line as the Pond edge picture and put its caption on the same row as the Pond edge caption. If the hero caption needs more height, let the hero picture end clearly short of that line, not just short by a few pixels. *Convention.*
3. **"Garden show gallery" could be any garden show.** It's the largest text on the page, and it doesn't say which show, where, or what year. A visitor who arrives from a search or a shared link can't tell if this is the event they went to. **Fix:** give the title or a line under it the show's name and date, for example "[Show name] 2026: highlights". *Defect (copy).*
