**Smaller things.**

Rebreak at "the first / ferry leaves," or set "just before the first ferry leaves," as one phrase (Bringhurst and Hochuli: break display lines at phrase boundaries).

They read as near-misses, not separate levels. Use one small size for all three and separate them by weight and colour only (Refactoring UI). The kicker and the caption especially should match.
