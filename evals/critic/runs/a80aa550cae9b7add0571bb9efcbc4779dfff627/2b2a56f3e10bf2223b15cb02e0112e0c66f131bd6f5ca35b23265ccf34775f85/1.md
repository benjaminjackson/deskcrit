**First read.** In version 1 the eye skips past the headline and lands on the three card titles, because "Perennials", "Fruit trees" and "Workshops" are set larger than "Hardy plants grown on our hillside". The page reads as a row of categories with a caption above it. In version 2 the eye lands on the headline first, then drops to the intro line and the cards. It reads as a nursery with something to say, then the ways in. Both feel calm, earthy and plain.

**What it's for.** My assumption: this is the home page of a small plant nursery. It should tell a gardener what makes these plants different (hardy, grown outdoors) and send them to plants or workshops.

**Verdict.** Version 2 does the job better. The only change is the headline size, and that change fixes an inverted hierarchy that undercut version 1. Version 2 still has no clear next step, and its headline wraps badly.

**What changed and why it matters**
Version 1's headline looks about 26 px, smaller than the 28 px or so card titles below it. The page's one claim, that these plants are hardy because they're grown on a cold hillside, ranks below three category labels. A visitor reads "Perennials / Fruit trees / Workshops" and could be on any garden centre's site. Version 2 sets the headline at about 60 px bold, clearly the top of the scale. The type now runs headline, card title, intro, body, with real steps between them, so every size has a job (Bringhurst; Refactoring UI). This is a defect fixed, not a matter of taste.

**What would move version 2 most**
1. **No call to action anywhere above the fold (defect).** The only things to act on are the small nav links and cards that don't look clickable: flat colour blocks with plain titles and no arrow, button or "Shop perennials →". A gardener persuaded by the headline has nowhere obvious to go. Fix: add one specific ask under the intro, such as "Browse 300 hardy perennials", and make the card titles look like links.
2. **The headline wraps to leave "hillside" alone on line two (convention).** The line breaks after "our", so the most distinctive word sits alone and the block looks unbalanced against the empty right half. Fix: widen the text box so it sets in one line, or break it deliberately, e.g. "Hardy plants / grown on our hillside".
3. **The image slots are flat colour fields.** Either the photos haven't loaded or these are placeholders (see below). If they ship like this, they're a defect: a nursery selling on how its plants look and grow is showing no plants. Fix: real photographs of the stock on the hillside, which would also prove the headline's claim.

**Smaller things.**
- The bottom third of the viewport is empty. Pull the next section up or tighten the vertical rhythm so the page doesn't look finished.
- "Sold in one and two litre pots": write "1 and 2 litre pots" so it scans as a quantity.
- The nav is dot-separated plain text at small size, and "Basket (0)" doesn't stand out as the commerce control. Give it a little more presence.

**What's working.**
- The intro copy lands its claim on the reader: plants "ready for a cold garden on the day they arrive" says what the buyer gets, not just how the plants are grown. Keep it.
- The restrained palette (sage, olive, dark green on an off-white ground) fits a nursery and stays legible.
- The three-column grid lines up cleanly with the logo and nav edges.

**Couldn't judge from a still.**
- Whether the colour blocks are placeholders or images that hadn't loaded. Check in a live browser.
- Whether the cards are clickable.

Better: version 2
