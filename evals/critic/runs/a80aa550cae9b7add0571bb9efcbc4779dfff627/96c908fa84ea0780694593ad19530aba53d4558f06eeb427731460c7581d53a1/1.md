I didn't crop the images. Both are 1280×800, about one screen, so I worked from the whole images.

**First read.** In both versions the eye goes to the inbox illustration, then the headline "No open conversations", then the body text, then the "Open customer list" button. It reads at once as an empty support inbox. It feels calm, tidy and a bit generic. The only thing that differs between the two is the illustration's colour: pale green in version 1, pale blue in version 2. The layout, type, copy, button and sidebar are the same in both.

**What it's for.** I'm assuming this is the empty state of a customer-support inbox. It should tell an agent that nothing needs answering and offer one useful next step.

**Verdict.** Both versions do the job equally well. The colour swap doesn't change what the viewer understands or does. If anything, green hints slightly more at "all clear", but that's taste and not enough to call either one better. Neither version has anything in the way: the message is clear and the next step is named.

**What would move it most**
1. **The button is the only action but it's styled like a minor one** (taste, leaning toward defect). "Open customer list" has a thin grey outline and sits on white, so it has less pull than the headline and the illustration. If starting a conversation from the customer list is the step you want agents to take, give it a solid fill so the eye lands there after the headline. If you'd rather agents just wait for new messages, keep it quiet. Either way, decide on purpose.

**Smaller things.**
- Sidebar: the only sign that you're in "Inbox" is bold text. A background tint or a marker on the active item would make "where am I" clearer. That matters most on an empty screen, where the sidebar is the agent's way to Waiting or Closed.
- The main pane has no header or page title, so the empty state carries all the orientation. Add a "Inbox" header above the pane.
- The body text's second sentence ("You can also start a conversation from a customer's profile") points to the profile, but the button opens the customer list. Make them match ("…from the customer list") so the text and the button point to the same place.

**What's working.**
- The headline says exactly what's happening ("No open conversations") rather than something cute, and the body text says what will appear here and how it gets there.
- The type scale is tight: one headline size, one body size and one button size, with clear steps between them.
- The illustration is muted and in proportion, so it frames the message without competing with it. That holds in either colour.

**Couldn't judge from a still.** How the button looks on hover and focus, and whether the illustration colour matches the product's brand colour. That should decide between green and blue.
