**What changed, and why it decides it**
1. **The headline's lines collide in version 2 (defect).** The descenders on the first line (the q, the p's and the g in "quiet people keeping") hit the l, i and h of "lighthouse" on the line below. The g's in "forgotten" and "lighthouse" cut into "Fair Skerry". Word shapes merge at the exact spot where a reader takes in the story's premise, so the headline reads as a dark mass and has to be read twice. It also looks like a broken page, which undercuts the care the rest of the page signals. Tight leading on large display type is a legitimate move, but only up to the point where descenders and ascenders touch (Bringhurst on leading). Version 2 goes past that point. Version 1's spacing keeps the three lines as separate lines while still reading as one block. Keep it, or tighten only slightly and stop before anything touches.
