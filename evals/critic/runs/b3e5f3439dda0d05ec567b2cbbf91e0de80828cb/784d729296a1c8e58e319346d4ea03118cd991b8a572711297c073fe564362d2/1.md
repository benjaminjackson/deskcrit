**First read.** This is a furniture store's order review step. The eye lands on "Review your order", moves down the three line items, then jumps across to the bold total and the green "Continue to payment" button. The two versions are identical except for the yellow delivery notice under the line items. Tile 1 sets it in normal upper and lower case. Tile 2 sets the whole paragraph in capitals, which makes it run to four lines instead of three and makes both cards about 23 px taller.

**Ranking**
1. Tile 1: The delivery notice reads as a normal sentence. It's the one paragraph a buyer has to read and act on before paying, and here they can read it easily.
2. Tile 2: The same notice in capitals is slower to read and reads as shouting. It's also a block buyers tend to skip, and this one holds the warning about measuring doorways.

**What decides it.** There's only one difference, and it's the notice at about y 380–505 on each version.

- **What changed:** Tile 1 sets four sentences of about 15 px text in mixed case over three lines. Tile 2 sets the same sentences in capitals at the same size, with no extra letter spacing, over four lines.
- **Why it matters to the buyer:** This box tells them two things they must act on:
  - Delivery is on a weekday between 8 am and 6 pm.
  - They should measure doorways and stairs first, because the drivers won't take doors off their hinges.
  
  Missing the second one leads to a failed delivery of a $940 table. Paragraphs in capitals lose the word shapes readers rely on, so they're read more slowly or skimmed. In tile 2 the box looks like legal small print and is the most likely thing on the page to be skipped.
- **Source:** Butterick, *Practical Typography*, "All caps": "Don't capitalize whole paragraphs". Hochuli, *Detail in Typography*: text in capitals "is particularly difficult to read".
- **Kind:** This is a defect, not taste. The text has to be read for the buyer to avoid a failed delivery, and tile 2 makes it harder to read.

Nothing in tile 2 makes up for this. The hierarchy, the summary card and the button are the same in both, and the bigger box doesn't make the warning stand out more. The yellow tint and amber bar already do that in both versions.

Best: tile 1
