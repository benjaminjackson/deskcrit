**First read.** This is a booking page for a bike repair shop. A rider should be able to see what a service costs, pick a drop-off day, and book. The two versions are the same pixel for pixel except for the fill of the "Book drop-off" button: tile 1 uses a deep teal and tile 2 a saturated blue. Everything else is identical at full size: layout, type, copy, price list and date options.

**Ranking**
1. Tile 1: ties with tile 2. The teal button is the strongest color on the page, it is clearly the main action, and its white label reads easily.
1. Tile 2: ties with tile 1. The blue button does the same job just as well, with slightly more contrast and a bit more pull.

**What decides it.** Nothing in the job does.
- **Contrast:** White bold text looks to clear the 4.5:1 WCAG 1.4.3 minimum on both. The teal looks about 5:1 and the blue about 6.5:1. These are estimates by eye, so check the exact hex values.
- **Dominance:** On both, the button is the only saturated area on a gray-and-white page. The eye goes from the headline to the button either way.
- **Brand fit (taste):** A shop called Spoke & Chain may sit more naturally with teal, and the blue looks more like a default UI blue. That is a brand question. If the shop has a brand color, use whichever one matches it.

Neither color fixes the issues both versions share:
- None of the date rows looks selected.
- Nothing links a price on the left to the booking on the right.
- The bottom third of the screen is empty.

The per-version crits should raise these.

Best: tie tile 1, tile 2
