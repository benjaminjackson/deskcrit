**First read.** This is the front page of a local news site, The Ashby Courier. I'm assuming its job is to show a reader in one glance what the top story is today and what else is worth a click. The two versions have the same five stories, the same masthead and the same type. Only the layout differs: tile 1 gives every story the same weight, and tile 2 makes the footbridge story the lead.

**Ranking**
1. Tile 2: the footbridge story is clearly the lead, so the page reads like a front page. The reader knows where to start and that this is the day's main news.
2. Tile 1: five stories the same size sit in one row, so nothing is seen first. The bottom half of the screen is left blank, and the page reads as a list rather than an edited front page.

**What decides it.**
- **One element leads (defect in tile 1).** In tile 1, all five images are the same size (about 211×150 px) and all five headlines are about 20 px bold. Nothing is clearly stronger, so the eye scans the row from left to right and the reader can't tell which story the paper thinks matters most. Tile 2 makes the footbridge image about 564×430 px and sets its headline at about 36 px, roughly twice the size of the other headlines. The other four stories step back into a 2×2 grid. That is the fix the entry gives: make one element clearly the first thing seen and set the rest back by size (layout.md "One element dominates"; Tschichold, *The New Typography*; Hofmann, *Graphic Design Manual*).
- **Where the empty space goes (defect in tile 1).** In tile 1 the stories end around y≈400, and the whole lower half of the 800 px screen is a blank white band. It looks like the page is still loading or has run out of stories, and the open space does nothing. Tile 2 fills the screen down to about y≈660, with the lead and the right-hand grid ending at nearly the same line. Its empty space sits below the content, where it belongs (layout.md "White space is gathered, not scattered"; Ruder, *Typography: A Manual of Design*).
- **What tile 2 still needs to fix.** These problems don't change the pick, but the full crit should raise them:
  - In the right-hand grid, each row of cards is followed by a gap of about 90 px under the short one-line summaries. That gap is much larger than the 24 px gutters, so the four cards read as two loose rows.
  - The bottom-row summaries end at about y≈660 and the lead's summary at about y≈650, a near-miss of about 10 px rather than a clear alignment.
  - The lead's summary is one line about 90 characters long, which runs past the 75-character limit if it ever wraps to a second line.

Best: tile 2
