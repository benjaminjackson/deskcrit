**What changed, and why it matters**
- **Version 1: the headings differ from the body by size alone** (a convention breach that costs something here). "What you will see" and "Opening times" look only about 6 px larger than the paragraphs under them, in the same face, weight and colour. Someone scanning for hours sees three paragraphs, not three sections. Dair (*Design with Type*, ch. 8 and 8a–8g) says a contrast has to be obvious at a glance, and that a small step in size should come with a change of weight, form, structure or colour.
- **Version 2 fixes exactly that.** It pairs bold weight with a change from serif to sans. The bold sans also matches the "Calder Museum" wordmark and the nav, so the headings, logo and nav now share one face. The serif stays where it reads best, in the paragraphs.
