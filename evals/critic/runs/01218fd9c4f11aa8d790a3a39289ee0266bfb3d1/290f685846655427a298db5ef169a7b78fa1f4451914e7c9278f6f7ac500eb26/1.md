**What changed, and why it matters**
- **Body text: script in v1, serif in v2 (defect in v1).** In v1, the paragraphs under the headline are about 60 words each in a connected brush script. Script is fine for a logo, but at body size and length the letters are hard to tell apart and the lines blur together. This is the copy that does the selling: the 1870 build, the oak floor, the caterer's kitchen, the cottage for two nights, bringing your own band and florist. It's what separates this barn from every other one. Version 2 uses a readable serif with open line spacing, so all of it gets through. Bringhurst and Butterick both keep decorative faces to short display use.
