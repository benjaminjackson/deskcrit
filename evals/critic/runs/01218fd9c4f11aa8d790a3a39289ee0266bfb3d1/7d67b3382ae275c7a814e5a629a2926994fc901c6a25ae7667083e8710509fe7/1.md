**What changed, and why it matters**
1. **Body text contrast (defect, WCAG 1.4.3).** In version 1 the three paragraphs are set in a light grey (roughly #808080) on white. That looks like it falls below the 4.5:1 minimum for normal-size text. Over six minutes of reading this tires the eyes, and it fails readers with low vision or anyone in bright light. A cyclist checking the route on a phone outdoors is a realistic reader here. Version 1 also inverts the hierarchy: the byline is darker than the article, so the metadata outweighs the content. Version 2's dark grey (roughly #555) looks well above 4.5:1, so the body reads as the main thing and the headline, byline and body now step down in a clear order. Run both colours through a contrast checker to confirm the exact ratios.
