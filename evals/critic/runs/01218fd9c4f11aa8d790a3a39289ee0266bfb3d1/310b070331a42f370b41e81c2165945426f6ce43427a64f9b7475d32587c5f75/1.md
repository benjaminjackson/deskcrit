**First read.** The eye goes to "Tell us about your project", then to the white card, then down the three fields. Then it stops. Under the message box sits "Send message" in plain body text, and it is hard to tell whether that is a button, a caption or leftover text. The page reads as a calm, competent contact page for a small design studio. It feels quiet and trustworthy, but it ends without a clear next step.

**What it's for.** My assumption: this is the contact page of a small agency's website. Its job is to get a prospective client to send a project enquiry.

**Verdict.** Almost all of it works, but the one control that matters doesn't look like a control. The submit action is set as bare text, so the form has no clear end and the page undercuts its own purpose.

**What would move it most**
1. **The submit action looks like a label, not a button. (Defect.)** "Send message", at the bottom left of the card, uses the same face, size, weight and colour as running text. It has no fill, no border and no underline, while even the footer links are underlined. A visitor who has just typed out their project has to stop and wonder whether that line is clickable, and some will hesitate or leave. On a page whose whole job is this one click, that is the biggest flaw. The rule that buttons need a shape, fill or border is common sense rather than something an approved source confirms; the NN/g article on flat UI (Moran) only shows that flat, text-like controls get less attention. Fix: make it a real primary button with a solid fill in the page's one accent colour (the link blue would work) and enough padding to read as the thing to press. It should be the strongest-looking element inside the card.

**Smaller things.**
- **Budget and timeline have nowhere to go.** The intro asks for "a budget range and a rough timeline", but the form only has a free-text box. Put that request as a hint under the Message label, where people will see it while typing, or add two optional short fields.
- **The nav doesn't show where you are.** "Contact" in the top-right nav looks the same as the other links. Give the current page a visible state such as bold, no underline or a marker (Nielsen 1).
- **The card doesn't line up with the header.** It ends at about x=1068, while the nav runs to about x=1224. The right edge lines up with nothing, so the leftover space on the right looks accidental. Either run the card to the content's right edge or let the two columns share the width more deliberately.
- **Required fields aren't marked.** If all three fields are required, one short line saying so is enough. If any are optional, put "(optional)" after their labels (Budiu, NN/g).
- **Field borders are pale.** The input borders look like light grey-blue on white and may fall below 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11). Measure them, and darken them if they fail.

**What's working.**
- **The headline speaks to the visitor.** "Tell us about your project" is about them, not about the studio, and the line under it makes a specific, checkable promise ("reply within two working days"). That beats a generic "Get in touch". Keep it.
- **The form layout is right.** Persistent labels sit above the fields in a single column, close to their own field and well clear of the one before. The grouping reads at a glance.
- **The page has one task.** There is no clutter competing with the form. Once the button exists, the white card on the grey ground will frame it.

**Couldn't judge from a still.** Focus rings on the fields, error and validation messages, and what the visitor sees after sending. The confirmation should repeat the two-day reply promise.
