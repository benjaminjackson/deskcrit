**First read.** In version 1 the eye lands on the masthead, then moves along five equal cards without stopping on any of them. It reads as a row of thumbnails, and nothing says what today's news is. In version 2 the eye goes from the masthead straight to the large blue image and the big headline "New footbridge over the Soar opens after two years of work", then on to the four smaller stories. Version 2 reads as a front page.

**What it's for.** I'm assuming this is the home page of a local news site, where readers should see the day's main story at once and then scan the rest.

**Verdict.** Version 2 does the job better. Version 1 gives the lead story the same size as the library's opening hours, so the page has no front. Version 2 fixes that with one clear lead and a supporting grid. What still holds version 2 back is loose spacing in the right-hand column and a bottom edge that doesn't line up.

**What changed, and why it matters**
1. **There is now a lead story.** This is a defect fixed. In version 1 all five cards use the same image size and headline size. The footbridge story only stands out because it wraps to three lines, so readers can't tell the big story from the filler. Version 2 gives the lead about four times the image area and a headline about twice the size. The page now ranks the news, which is a newspaper's main job.
2. **The type scale now has real steps.** This is a convention: Bringhurst's point that a type scale needs clear steps between sizes. Version 1 has three sizes: masthead, one headline size, and the dek (the one-line summary under each headline). Version 2 adds a lead headline size well apart from the card headlines, so each size has an obvious job.
3. **The deks have room.** In version 1 the five columns are narrow, and lines like "Keeper saves two in the shoot-/out." break awkwardly. In version 2 the four small cards each fit their dek on one line.

**What would move version 2 further**
1. **The right-hand column has a big hole in the middle.** This is a defect. Under "Traders pay half rent until March" and "Forty plots added at Glebe Farm" there's roughly 90 px of white before the second row of images. That gap is much larger than the gutters (the space between columns), so the four cards look like two loose pairs rather than one block next to the lead. Close that gap until it matches the gutters, or make the small images taller so the two rows fill the lead image's height.
2. **The bottom edges don't line up.** This is taste. The lead's dek ends at about y=650 and the bottom-right deks end at about y=660, with the two headlines starting at different heights. It's close enough to look like a mistake. Line up the lead headline with the second-row headlines, or the last lines of text with each other.
3. **The lead dek is too long a line.** This is a convention: Bringhurst puts comfortable line length at 45 to 75 characters. "Walkers can now cross from Quay Street to the meadows without the ring road." runs about 80 characters across roughly 560 px in small grey type, which is thin for the story that's meant to carry the page. Set it a step larger or narrower.

**Smaller things.**
- In both versions the deks sit very close under the headlines. Add a few px so the headline reads as its own unit.
- The navigation ("News · Sport · Business · Weather") is small and grey. It looks faint, so check its contrast against WCAG's 4.5:1 for normal text.
- The bottom band of the page is empty in both versions. It probably needs a second row or section, or the frame should end sooner.

**What's working.**
- The serif headlines against sans-serif deks give the page a newspaper voice. Keep that.
- The copy is concrete and local: "Forty plots added at Glebe Farm" and "Open from ten until four from November" each tell readers something they can act on. Keep writing deks this way.
- In version 2 the lead runs half the width beside a 2×2 grid. That's a clear, conventional front-page structure.

**Couldn't judge from a still.**
- The images are flat colour blocks, so these are probably placeholders. Real photos will change how much weight each card carries, so check the hierarchy again once they're in.
- I can't see how the layout reflows on narrower screens.

Better: version 2
