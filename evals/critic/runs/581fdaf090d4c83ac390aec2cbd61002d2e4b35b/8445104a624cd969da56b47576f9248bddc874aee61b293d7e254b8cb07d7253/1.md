**What would move it most**
1. **The two columns run on different baselines.** Every line in the right column sits about 10 px, close to half a line, below its nearest neighbour in the left column. This is visible from "Stallholders arrive…" at the top all the way down to "the last boat." Across the gutter the lines never form rows, so the spread reads as two separate texts placed side by side rather than one woven page. The offset is also just large enough to look like a mistake rather than a choice. Müller-Brockmann's grid puts every column's lines on shared baselines. Fix: snap the right column's first line to the left column's line interval, and give the subheads and the space around them whole multiples of that interval. Then "Going home" and "The market quay" land on the grid too, and both columns stay in step from top to bottom. *Convention*, and here it costs some of the page's polish.

**Smaller things.**
- The first paragraph under each heading ("The first ferry…", "The market quay is…", "In the evening…") is indented. A subhead already marks where the paragraph starts, so set those three lines flush left and indent only the paragraphs that follow (Bringhurst 2.3.1).
- "flowers." stands alone as the last line of the market quay paragraph. Re-break that paragraph or edit it a little so the last line carries more than one word (Bringhurst 2.4.2).
