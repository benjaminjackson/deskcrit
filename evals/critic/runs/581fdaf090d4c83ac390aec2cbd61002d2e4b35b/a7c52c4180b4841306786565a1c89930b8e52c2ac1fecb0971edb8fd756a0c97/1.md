**First read.** The eye lands on "Library opening hours" at top left, then drops down three tidy bold-labelled blocks. In two seconds it reads as a plain, calm hours page for a small library service. Then you start reading the times, and that is where it slows down: "nine to eight", "ten to four", "ten to two" are spelled out as words, and the third block has no times at all.

**What it's for.** My assumption: a web page or panel that lets local residents quickly check when each branch is open, so they can decide when to go.

**Verdict.** The layout is clean and the hierarchy is right, but the page fails at its one job: the times are written in a way that is slow to scan and can be read wrongly, and some of what a visitor needs to plan a trip is missing. Fix how the times are written before you touch anything else.

**What would move it most**
1. **The times are spelled out in words, and two of them read as clock times** (all six time lines). "Saturday, ten to four" and "Saturday, ten to two" are also how British English says 3:50 and 1:50, so a reader can take them as single moments, not as opening hours. Even read correctly, "nine to eight" has no am or pm, and words make you read every line: you can't glance down the column and pick out the numbers. Opening hours are exactly what people act on, so a line that can be read two ways sends someone to a closed door. Hours listings are conventionally written in numerals with a range dash, and this page breaks that convention for no gain. *Defect* (Nielsen 4, consistency and standards; Krug, *Don't Make Me Think*, on pages built for scanning). Fix: write numerals with an en dash and a clear day/time split, e.g. "Mon–Fri 9am–8pm". Set the days and the hours in two aligned columns so the times line up and you can compare branches at a glance.
2. **The Mobile library block has no hours, and nothing says where or when anything is closed** (third block, and the gaps in the first two). "Wednesday at the market square" tells me the day but not when to turn up, which is the one fact I came for. Closed days, such as Sunday at both branches and Monday at Harbour, show up only as missing lines, so the reader has to work out the closures from what isn't there. There are also no addresses, so "Harbour branch" means nothing to a newcomer. *Defect* (common sense: the page should give the visitor everything they need to make the trip, without guessing). Fix: add times to both mobile stops, state closed days in words ("Sunday: closed"), and put a short address or location under each branch name.

**What's working.**
- The clear three-level hierarchy: a bold ~28px heading, bold ~18px labels, and regular ~18px lines. The levels differ by weight as well as size, so each branch name is easy to find without shouting. Keep it.
- The spacing between groups is clearly larger than the line spacing within a group, so the three locations separate without any rules or boxes. Keep it.
- High-contrast near-black on white, in one plain face, flush left. It's calm and readable, and right for a civic utility page.
