**What would move it most**
1. **Body text contrast (the change between versions). Defect.** In version 1 the three paragraphs (rows ~290–630) are set in a light grey that looks like it falls under 4.5:1 on white. At this size that is normal text, so it needs 4.5:1 (WCAG 2.2, SC 1.4.3). Readers with low vision, readers on a dim or sunlit screen, and anyone reading for six minutes pay for it in strain. It also turns the hierarchy upside down: the byline is darker than the content it introduces. Version 2 darkens the body to a mid-dark grey that looks well above 4.5:1. The paragraphs now carry their proper weight under the headline and still stay softer than it. Keep version 2's value, and confirm the exact ratio with a contrast checker on the real hex values.
