For example, the right column's "In the evening…" sits between two lines of the left column instead of level with one.
   - **Why it matters:** a reader glancing across the 70 px gutter sees two rows of text slightly out of step. That is what makes a well-set page look not quite finished.
   - **Fix:** put every vertical space on the body line interval (about 25 px):
     - Give headings, including the space above and below them, a whole number of lines.
     - Start both columns on the same first line, either both on a heading or both on body text set level with the heading.
   - **Check:** measure the line height and the heading margins, and make each heading block (margin above, heading, margin below) add up to a whole number of body lines.
