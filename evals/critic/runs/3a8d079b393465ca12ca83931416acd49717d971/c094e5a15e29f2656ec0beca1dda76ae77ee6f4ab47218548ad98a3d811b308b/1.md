**What it's for.** My assumption: it tells local people, both those who might need a parcel and those who might give or volunteer, how the food bank works week to week.

**Verdict.** Version 2 does the job better. The only change is that the two body paragraphs go from centred to flush left, and that fixes the one real fault in version 1. Version 2 is a sound, readable page. What holds it back now is that it explains the service and then gives the reader nothing to do.

**What changed, and why it matters**
- **Version 1 centres two four-line paragraphs** (y≈215–440) between a left-aligned heading and left-aligned stats. Every line starts somewhere different, so the reader has to search for the start of each of eight lines. These are exactly the paragraphs people read closely: when the van comes, how to get referred, home delivery. The centring also breaks the page's only strong line, the 96 px left edge, so the page zig-zags left → centre → left. This is a convention breach with a real cost (Tschichold, *The New Typography*; Bringhurst, 2.1.3). Version 2 fixes it by setting the paragraphs flush left on the same edge as the heading and stats, so the text reads easily and the page holds together.

**What would move version 2 most**
1. **No next step for either reader.** The second paragraph tells someone in need that they need a referral from a doctor, school, or housing office, and that a driver can bring the parcel. It ends there: no link to how to get referred, no list of the four centres, no way to ask for delivery. A donor reading "412 parcels last month" gets no ask either. The only way forward is the small nav at the top right. *Defect:* the person most likely to read this paragraph is left to hunt. Fix: end the text with one specific ask aimed at the person in need, e.g. "Find your nearest collection centre" or "Ask about home delivery". If a second ask fits, make it smaller, e.g. "Drop food at Carver Road on Tuesday or Friday".

**Smaller things**
- Body lines run about 80–85 characters, a little over the 45–75 range (Bringhurst, 2.1.2). Narrow the column by roughly 10%. This applies to both versions.
- The stats (412 parcels last month, 96 volunteers, 4 collection points) have no comparison, so the reader can't tell whether 412 is a lot or a growing need (Few). Add one reference point, such as last year's figure or "up from 300 in May".
- Nothing in the nav marks the current page (Laubheimer, NN/g, "Menu-Design Checklist"). Underline or bold the active item. This applies to both versions.

**What's working.**
- The copy is concrete and local: Tuesday and Friday, Carver Road, three days of meals, four community centres, a driver to your door. It says who does what and when, so a stranger can follow it.
- The type is clear: a bold sans heading, a serif for reading, and green figures for the stats. Three distinct levels and a restrained green-and-white palette suit a community service.
- In version 2, one left edge runs from heading to text to stats, which gives the page its order.

**Couldn't judge from a still.** Whether the nav marks the current page on hover or focus, and how the column reflows on a phone.
