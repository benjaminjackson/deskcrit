**Verdict.** It doesn't quite do its job yet. The parts are clean and readable, but the shifted Billing card makes the page look broken, and the sidebar points to a different page from the one on screen. Fix those two and this is a solid, unremarkable settings page, which is what it should be.

**What would move it most**
1. **The Billing card is out of line (defect).** The other three cards share a left edge at about x=304 and a right edge at about x=1216. The Billing card starts at about x=332 and ends at about x=1243, so it overhangs the header's right margin. The shift is too small to read as a choice and too large to miss. Viewers read it as a bug, and on the card that holds money and renewal dates that hurts trust most (Arnheim: a placement that is neither clearly here nor clearly there looks unfinished; Müller-Brockmann: every block takes its edges from one grid). It also breaks the pattern the other three cards set, so it looks like it came from another build (Gerstner, *Designing Programmes*). Fix: put it back on the same edges and width as the other cards. If the offset is meant to mark the current section, drop it and use a real selected treatment instead, such as a border or tint, not a nudge.
2. **The sidebar and the page disagree about where you are (defect).** The sidebar marks "Billing" as current with a tint, blue text, and bold, but the page heading is "Account settings" and shows Profile, Notifications, Billing, and Sessions as equals. The two lists don't match either: Members, Integrations, and Security are in the sidebar but have no card, and Sessions has a card but no sidebar item. A user can't tell whether they're on the Billing page, an overview, or something in between. They also can't tell whether "Sessions" is the same thing as "Security" (Nielsen heuristic 1, visibility of system status; Norman, *Design of Everyday Things*, ch. 2, conceptual model). Fix: decide what this screen is. If it's an overview, nothing in the sidebar should be marked current, or add an "Overview" item and mark that. If it's the Billing page, the heading and content should be Billing. Either way, use one set of names for the sections in both places.

**Smaller things.**
- The Sessions copy, "You are signed in on three now", drops the noun. Restore it: "You are signed in on three devices now." (Copy, common sense.)
- "Your plan renews on 1 March" has no year. Renewal dates are something users hold you to, so add the year if the renewal falls in the next calendar year. Billing ends on a fact ("the address on file") without saying what it means for the user. Consider whether the line should say where they can change that address.

**What's working.**
- The type scale is clear: page title, card title, and description are three distinct steps, with weight doing the work between title and description. Keep it.
- The palette is restrained, with one blue used only for actions and the selected state, and neutral grays everywhere else. That keeps the four buttons obvious without any of them shouting.
- The button labels name what happens (Edit, Manage, View invoices, Review), not a generic "Open". That tells users what they'll get before they click.

**Couldn't judge from a still.**
- Focus indicators on the sidebar items and card buttons. Check them in a live browser.
