**What would move it most**

1. **There's nothing here a visitor can act on.** *Defect, copy.* The only line about visiting is "open to visitors on summer weekends", in the middle of paragraph 2, and "Tickets are sold at the cottage by the car park" at the end. A stranger reading this can't tell which months count as summer, what hours the tower is open, what a ticket costs, or where the car park is. "Summer weekends" can mean June to August or any warm weekend, and "on a clear morning" suggests a morning opening that the page never confirms. Someone who drives out on a guess and finds it shut will blame the page. Direction: give the visit facts (the dates or months, the opening hours, the price, and where it is) their own short block, set apart from the history and easy to scan, before or right under the story paragraphs.
