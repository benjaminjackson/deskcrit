**What would move it most**

That is well past the 45 to 75 range Bringhurst gives, with about 66 as the ideal. On lines this long the eye has to travel back across the whole column and can land on the wrong next line. The loose 1.65 line height (`line_height_ratio`) helps, but not enough. This is the one thing that makes reading the piece harder. Narrow the column to about 600 px, or raise the body text to 18 px at the same width. Either brings it to roughly 65 to 75 characters. *Convention (Bringhurst, 2.1.2), with a real reading cost.*
