**First read.** All six screens read as one product. They share the same sidebar, title block, content edge and teal accent. Moving from Today to Alerts, nothing jumps: the frame stays still and only the content area changes. The only place the set loosens is the corner style of the white containers.

**Verdict.** The set holds together. In the full-size screens no single tile breaks a pattern the others keep. The one difference is small and is split between two pairs of screens, not owned by one.

**Breaks**
No break found.

**Smaller things.**
- **Container corners (tiles 1 and 2 against tiles 3, 5 and 6).** The data tables on Today and Patients have square corners. The stat cards (tiles 1 and 3), room cards (tile 3), settings list (tile 5) and alerts list (tile 6) all have rounded corners, about 8 px. Even on tile 1, the stat cards are rounded and the table under them is square. A viewer is unlikely to notice. Still, a bordered white panel is one kind of element, so pick one radius and use it on tables too (Refactoring UI: consistent border radius). This is a convention, and it costs little.

**What's working.**
- **The frame is fixed.** The sidebar is 223 px wide. "Haven Clinic" and the six nav items sit at the same heights on every screen, and the active item gets the same pale teal fill and bold teal label. The viewer always knows where they are, and the nav never shifts under the cursor.
- **One content edge and one type scale.** Every title starts at x=264 and y≈54, with its gray subtitle at y≈90. Content spans 264 to 1240 on every screen. The stat cards on tiles 1 and 3 and the room cards on tile 3 share one three-column split, with columns starting at 264, 595 and 925. Tile 4's form uses its own two-column split, which fits a form. Section headings ("Next appointments", "All patients", "Reply to R. Dubois", "Reminders", "Today and yesterday") are all the same size and weight. That gives three clear levels that repeat everywhere (Müller-Brockmann, *Grid Systems*).
- **One button and one badge style.** The filled teal primary button with an outlined secondary button is about 42 px tall on both tile 2 (Add patient and Import list) and tile 4 (Send reply and Cancel). The status pills on tiles 1 and 2 match, and teal is the only accent in the set. Once a user learns which button is the main action on one screen, that holds on every other.

Breaks: none
