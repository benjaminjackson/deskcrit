**First read.** All five screens read as one product. The sidebar, the 264 px content edge, the title and subtitle pair, the orange accent and the gray canvas are the same on every screen. Pantry (tile 3) is the one place it stops feeling like the same build: its icons switch from thin outlines to solid shapes, in the sidebar and in its own cards.

**Verdict.** Layout, type and color hold together well. The one real break is the icon style on tile 3.

**Breaks**
1. **Icon style, outline vs. filled**: tiles 1, 2, 4 and 5 keep it; tile 3 breaks it. On every other screen the sidebar icons are thin outlines in gray, and the active one is an outline in orange. On Pantry, the Recipes house, the Shopping person and the Settings sun are solid gray shapes, while Plan and Pantry stay as outlines. The category cards on the same screen mix them too: a solid orange house next to outline "Batch cooking" and "Baking" icons. Someone moving between screens sees the sidebar change even though they didn't touch it. That reads as a different build or an unfinished screen, and solid icons look heavier, so on this one screen Recipes, Shopping and Settings look more important than Pantry, the screen they are on. This is a **defect**: it breaks one look per kind of element (Nielsen, consistency and standards). The fix is to use the outline set from the other four screens on Pantry, in both the sidebar and the cards.

**Smaller things.**
- Tiles 2 and 4 use the same button label for different jobs. "Add recipe" is the main action on Meal plan, and also the submit button for a servings, diet and dislikes form on Shopping list. Someone who learned the label on Plan will expect it to do the same thing on Shopping. Give the Shopping button a label that names what that form does.
- Tile 5: the settings list stops at about x 824, while every other screen fills the column out to x 1240. A narrower list may be on purpose, to keep each toggle close to its label. If it is, consider matching the right edge to the two-column form on tile 4 (x 736) so the narrow width looks like part of the grid rather than a one-off.

**What's working.**
- The page header is exactly the same on all five screens: title at 264 px, the same size, the subtitle under it, and content starting at about y 128. Every screen opens the same way.
- Shared parts match where they repeat. The stat cards on tiles 1 and 3 and the week table on tiles 1 and 2 are pixel-identical in size, column positions and diet tags. The primary and secondary buttons on tiles 2 and 4 match in size, radius and color.
- One accent color does every job: the active nav item, the primary button, the tags and the toggles. Orange always means selected or the main action.

Breaks: tile 3
