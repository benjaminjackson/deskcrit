**First read.** Seen together, the five screens look like one product. They all have the same white sidebar with "Fernhill" at the top, the same five nav items with icons, and the same pale green pill for the active page. Content starts at the same left edge on every screen (x≈264 px) and ends at the same right edge (x≈1240 px). The palette is the same serif-and-moss-green throughout. The one screen that feels different is Settings: its header looks smaller and sits higher than on the other four.

**Verdict.** The set holds together well. There is one real break: Settings (tile 5) uses a smaller page title and a smaller section heading than the other four screens, so the header steps down when you arrive there.

**Breaks**
1. **Page title and section heading sizes.** Tiles 1–4 keep the pattern; tile 5 breaks it. On Shop, Orders, Plants, and Customers, the page title has a cap height of about 32 px at full size, and the subtitle baseline sits near y=94. The section headings ("To pack", "All orders", "Low stock", "Add a customer") are all one size, at about 24 px. On Settings, the cap height of "Settings" is about 23 px, so the title is roughly a quarter smaller. Its subtitle sits higher, near y=87. The section heading "Shop" is visibly smaller too, at about 18–19 px against about 24 px elsewhere.
   - **What the user notices:** Moving from any other page to Settings, the header shrinks and jumps up a few pixels while the sidebar stays put. The page feels like it came from another template. "Shop" also stops reading as the same kind of heading as "To pack" or "Low stock".
   - **Why it matters:** One size per level is how a type scale tells the reader what each level is (Bringhurst on type scale). Here the title and the section heading each have two sizes across the set.
   - **Fix:** Use the shared page-header and section-heading styles on Settings so its title, subtitle, and first heading match the other screens in size and position.
   - **Kind:** Convention (consistency across screens).

**Smaller things.** None.

**What's working.**
- **Sidebar:** The width (about 224 px), the item order, the icons, and the active-state pill are identical on every screen. You always know where you are, and nothing moves under the cursor when you switch pages.
- **Margins and grid:** The left and right content edges are the same everywhere. Card rows on Shop and Plants split into the same three columns. The Orders and Shop tables share one row style, and the status badges look the same on both.
- **Buttons:** There is one primary style (solid green) and one secondary style (outlined green), and both screens that have buttons (Orders, Customers) use them the same way.
