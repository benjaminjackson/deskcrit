**First read.** The eye goes to "Tell us about your project", then across to the white form card, down the three fields, and stops at the bottom of the card with nothing to press. "Send message" looks like a caption, not a button. It reads as a small studio's contact page: calm and tidy, but not finished.

**What it's for.** My assumption: this is the contact page of a design or agency studio, and it should get a prospective client to send a project enquiry.

**Verdict.** The page doesn't do its job yet. Its one action, sending the form, has no button. Everything else is competent, so fixing that is most of the work.

**What would move it most**
1. **The submit action is plain text** (defect). At the foot of the card, "Send message" is set in the same face, size, weight and near-black as the body text. It has no fill, no border and no underline. A visitor who has filled in three fields looks for a button and finds a label. Some will hesitate, and some will think the form is broken. This is the one moment the page exists for, and right now it is its weakest element. Its hit area is also probably just the glyphs, which is a small target. Fix: make it a real button with a fill in the one accent colour the page already uses (the link blue), sized clearly larger than a line of text. It should be the most clickable-looking thing on the screen (Material Design and Apple HIG on button emphasis; NN/g, Moran, "Flat UI Elements Attract Less Attention and Cause Uncertainty").

**Smaller things.**
- Top nav: "Contact" is drawn exactly like Work, Services and About, so nothing shows which page you're on. Mark the current item with one cue, such as bold or a bar (Nielsen heuristic 1; Laubheimer, NN/g "Menu-Design Checklist").
- Form: no field is marked required or optional. A visitor can't tell whether Message can be left blank. Put an asterisk on the required fields and explain it once, or add "(optional)" after the optional ones (Budiu, NN/g "Marking Required Fields in Forms").
- Field borders: the grey outlines on white look close to or under 3:1. Measure them against WCAG 1.4.11 and darken them if they fall short. Then the fields hold their shape for low-vision users.
- The hint "Include a budget range and a rough timeline" is in the left column, about 450 px from the Message box it's about. People read it once and forget it by the time they type. Repeat it as hint text under the Message label, or use it as the box's prompt (common sense; Nielsen heuristic 10).
- Grid: the card's right edge (around x=1068) lines up with nothing else. The nav runs to around x=1224, which leaves a dead strip on the right that looks unplanned. Either run the card out to the nav's right edge or set both columns on a grid the header shares (Müller-Brockmann, *Grid Systems*).

**What's working.**
- The intro copy is concrete. "We read every message and reply within two working days" makes a promise someone can hold the studio to, and the budget/timeline line tells the visitor exactly what a useful message looks like. Keep both.
- The hierarchy is clear. One bold headline, a quieter grey paragraph, and bold field labels sitting tight above their fields. Nothing competes with the form.
- The palette is restrained: neutral greys plus one link blue. That leaves room for the submit button to be the only filled colour on the page.

**Couldn't judge from a still.** Focus rings on the fields and the button, and the error and success states after submitting. Those states decide whether this form actually works, so check them live.
