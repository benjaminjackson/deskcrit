**Verdict.** The top half works: a clear question, a sentence that tells you what saving will do, and one obvious primary button. The button row is the weak part. The one action that destroys work, "Discard changes", looks exactly like a harmless side option. It sits in the middle of the row, between the export link and the cancel button, so it is easy to hit by accident and gives no warning about what you lose.

**What would move it most**
1. **"Discard changes" looks harmless and sits where hands go by mistake** (defect). It is a plain blue text button, the same as "Export as file", and it sits right next to "Keep editing". Because the two blue text buttons look alike, the viewer reads them as a pair of minor extras. Nothing marks "Discard" as the choice that throws away the new rehearsal times. Someone who wants to back out reaches for the area left of "Save" and can land on "Discard" instead of "Keep editing". Apple HIG ("Alerts", "Buttons") and Material ("Dialogs") both say to set destructive actions apart from safe ones, style them as destructive, and name the specific outcome. Direction:
   - Style it as destructive (usually red text).
   - Move it away from the cancel button, for example to the far left of the row, apart from the safe actions.
   - Name what is lost, such as "Discard new times".
   - Better still, offer undo after discarding instead of relying on this one click.
2. **"Export as file" doesn't belong in this choice** (convention). The dialog asks one question: save, keep editing, or throw the changes away. Exporting answers a different question, so it adds a fourth option for the eye to read and reject. It is also what pushes the row to four buttons, more than the usual two or three in platform dialogs (Material, Apple HIG). Rare side tasks belong behind a control in the editor, not in the decision row (Nielsen, "Progressive Disclosure"). Direction: remove it from the dialog. If people really do need it at this moment, make it a quiet link away from the button row, not a peer of the three real choices.
