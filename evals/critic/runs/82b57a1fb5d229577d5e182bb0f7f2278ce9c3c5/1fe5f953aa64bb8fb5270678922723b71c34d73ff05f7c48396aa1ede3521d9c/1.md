**Verdict.** This doesn't do its job yet. The headings and blurbs are clear and well set, but the cards stop just where a visitor's next question starts (when, how much, how do I go) and leave about half of every card as empty colour. It looks like a mock-up with the bottom half missing.

**What would move it most**

"Tuesday evenings" has no time, "every morning from six" has no end time, and "Sunday afternoons" is open-ended. Krug's test for a page is that you can tell what it is, what you can do and where to click within seconds, and this passes the first part only. *Defect.* Fix: give each card a concrete line of facts (day, time range, price or "free with membership") and a clearly styled action that names the act, such as "Book a Tuesday class" or "See lane times". Use the same style on all three so none is louder than the others.

**What's working**
- The type is right for the job: a large, tight, bold sans heading over regular-weight body text at a comfortable size. That's two clear levels, separated by weight as well as size, and white on all three colours looks comfortably readable (about 5:1 or better).
- The blurbs are good copy. Each one names who it's for and one concrete, reassuring detail: "a coach in the water beside you", "three speeds to choose from", "floats and toys for the children". Keep that voice when you add times and prices.
- The cards are consistent, with equal widths, equal gutters, matching inner padding and headings that share a baseline, so the three read as peer choices.
