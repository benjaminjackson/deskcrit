**What would move it most**
1. **The opening quote mark pushes the first line in.** The "“" sits on the shared left edge at about x=100, so "The" starts about 37 px to the right of "just", "leaves" and "the" below it. Everything else on the page (the label, the quote, the name, the caption) hangs on that one vertical line, and that line is what gives the layout its quiet order. At display size, the indent on line one is the only thing that breaks it, and it reads as a stumble right where the eye enters the piece. Fix: hang the opening quote mark in the left margin so the "T" of "The" lines up with the other lines at x=100. At this size the closing mark can stay as it is. This is common sense here, not a confirmed published rule: the hanging-punctuation entry's source is unverified, but its caveat says the defect grows with type size, and at about 80 px it is plainly visible.
