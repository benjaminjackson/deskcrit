**New findings**
1. **The weekday bars compare totals over a month where the weekdays don't occur equally often** (the bar chart, Tuesday and Wednesday bars). The source line says these are orders delivered in September 2026. That month starts on a Tuesday and has 30 days, so it has five Tuesdays and five Wednesdays but only four of every other day. If the bars are monthly totals, which is how the source line reads, then Tuesday and Wednesday each get an extra day's orders, roughly 25% more than they would otherwise. That makes the midweek climb look steeper than it really is, and the owner could staff or bake for a pattern that is partly a calendar effect. Bars should compare like with like (Tufte, *The Visual Display of Quantitative Information*, on not letting the data's makeup distort what you see; Few). The fix is to plot the average orders per delivery day, or orders per occurrence of each weekday, and say so in the axis or source line. If the bars are already averages, say that in the source line so nobody has to guess. This is a defect.
