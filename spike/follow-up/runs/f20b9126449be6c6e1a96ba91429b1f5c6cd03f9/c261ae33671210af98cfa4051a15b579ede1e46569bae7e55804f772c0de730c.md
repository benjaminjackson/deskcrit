**New findings**
1. **"Learn to swim" doesn't say it's for adults.** In the top left of the orange card, the headline reads as the pool's general swimming lessons. At most pools that usually means children's lessons. Only the body copy ("Small adult classes…") tells you it's for grown-ups. A parent skimming the three headlines could take this card as the kids' option and skip "Family hour", or read the blurb and leave thinking there are no lessons for children. A nervous adult beginner, the person this card is for, might also pass over it as not meant for them. The headlines are the largest text on the cards and what people scan, so they need to name the audience. Put "adults" into the headline or a short line just above it, such as "Adult lessons" or "Learn to swim as an adult", and keep the warm blurb underneath. *Convention.*
