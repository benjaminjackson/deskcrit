1. **Membership is mentioned, but there's no way to join.** In the middle of the paragraph (lines 4–6, "a membership of just over two thousand people"), the text tells the reader the trust has members. It never says how to become one, what it costs, or what members get. A neighbor who walks the paths but can't give up a Saturday has nothing to do here. For that reader the section ends with no next step, even though membership is probably the easier yes for most people. Add a short second ask next to the work-day block, such as "Become a member" with a link. Leave the price and benefits to whatever the trust actually offers. *Taste*: the brief doesn't say whether membership is a goal of this section.
