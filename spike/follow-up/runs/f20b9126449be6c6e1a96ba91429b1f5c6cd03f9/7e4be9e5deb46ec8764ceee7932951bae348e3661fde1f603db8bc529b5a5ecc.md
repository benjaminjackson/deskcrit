**New findings**
1. **The site has no header or navigation (convention).** The top band of the screen, about 0–120 px across the full width, is empty cream. There's no logo, no menu, and no links to pages like bread, hours, or contact. The crit points out the missing call to action after the subhead. This is a different gap. A visitor who wants anything other than that one action, such as the full range, prices, or wholesale, has nothing to click and can't tell what else the site holds. "Hartley's Bakery" appears only as the small eyebrow label, so the screen doesn't look like a site's home page. People expect a site's navigation along the top, and leaving it out needs a clear gain (Nielsen, Jakob's law). The screen should also show how the site is organized (Norman, *The Design of Everyday Things*, conceptual model). Fix: add a slim top bar with the bakery's name or mark on the left at the same 110-px edge and a few plain links on the right. Only the maker knows which pages exist. Keep the bar quiet so the headline still dominates.
