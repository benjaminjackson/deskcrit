**New findings**
1. **The conversion-rate delta contradicts the other two cards** (*defect*). This is in the KPI row at the top, in the third card under "2.12%". The card says "+0.1 pts vs previous 12 weeks" in green, which reads as good news. But sign-ups grew 2.4% while visits grew 6.1%. If conversion means sign-ups ÷ visits, that rate has to have gone down. The current numbers give 3,912 ÷ 184,220 ≈ 2.12%, which matches the card. Working back from the stated growth, the previous period was about 3,820 ÷ 173,630 ≈ 2.20%. That's a drop of about 0.08 pts, not a gain of 0.1. A reader who checks the arithmetic, or simply notices that sign-ups grew slower than visits, will stop trusting every number on the page. Meanwhile a reader who doesn't check gets the wrong story: the dashboard's one warning sign is shown in green as a win. To fix it, check how the delta is calculated. If it's a bug, show the real change with the color for a decline. If conversion is defined some other way, such as a weekly average or a different denominator, the maker should state that definition in the label so the three cards stop contradicting each other.
