Someone on the north bank in the evening can't tell from this page whether they still have a way across. If they guess wrong, they're stuck on the wrong side. Say that the service runs both ways, and give the last departure from each landing separately. Only you know whether those two times differ.

But the fare and the way to pay (cash on board, card, ticket from the shelter, or whatever applies) appear nowhere.
