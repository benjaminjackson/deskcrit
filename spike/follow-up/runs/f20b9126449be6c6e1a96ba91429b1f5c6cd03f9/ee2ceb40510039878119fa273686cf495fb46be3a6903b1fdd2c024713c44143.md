**New findings**
1. **The first step has no heading (defect).** At the top left, the "Choose a spot…" paragraph sits right under the title with no subhead, while the soil, plants and water steps each get one. Picking the site is the first step of the sequence, but someone scanning down the left edge sees only three headings: soil, plants, watering. Coming back later to check how much sun the spot needs, they won't find it by heading, and the paragraph reads like an intro rather than an instruction. Give it a subhead styled like the other three (something about the site) and leave the same gap above it. That also gives the page the four headings the step sequence implies.
2. **The guide never says how big to make the bed (taste).** It's the "Mark the corners with sticks and a length of string" line in the first paragraph. A beginner is told to mark out corners but gets no size, and in a piece called "Planning a vegetable bed," size is the main planning decision. They have to guess before they can do the very first thing they're told to do. Add a starting size or a rule of thumb next to the corner-marking line. The actual figure is the maker's call.
