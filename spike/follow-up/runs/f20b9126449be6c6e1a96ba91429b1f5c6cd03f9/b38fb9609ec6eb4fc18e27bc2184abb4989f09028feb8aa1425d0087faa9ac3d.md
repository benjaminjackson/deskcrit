**New findings**
1. **The voice changes mid-paragraph from "it" to "we"** (defect, copy). In the body text, lines 1–7 describe the charity from outside: "The Moorside Trust looks after…", "It was set up…", "Today it has four staff…". Then line 8 switches to "We provide tools, gloves…" with no warning. The reader suddenly has to work out who "we" is. Is it the trust, the staff, or the volunteers? The switch also makes the paragraph read like two pieces of copy pasted together. This matters more because the crit's main fix is to split the block in two. Pick one voice per part and keep to it. The volunteer part works well in "we", since it's speaking directly to the reader. The "who the trust is" part can use "we" too ("We look after eleven miles of footpath…") or stay in the third person, but don't mix the two inside a section.
