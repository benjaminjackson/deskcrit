**New findings**
1. **The byline has no date, so the story's timeline can't be placed.** The byline (under the headline, about y=250) reads only "By Theo Marchetti · Photographs by the residents". The intro starts with "Ten years ago…" and "Today forty households…", and the second paragraph says the reply "took four months". All of these count from a "now" the page never gives. A reader can't tell whether the garden is still running, when "a season" was, or whether "forty households" is still true. That matters more here because the piece is about a place someone might go and visit. *Convention.* The fix: put the publication date in the byline line, and an "updated" date if the piece has been revised. Leave the exact wording to the maker.
2. **"Subscribe" is styled like a section link.** In the top-right nav, "Subscribe" has the same weight, color and dot separator as "Features · Food · Places". But it's an action (paying or signing up), not a place to read. It gets lost as the last item in a list of sections, and anyone scanning for sections gets an item that isn't one. *Convention.* The fix: split it from the section links with a larger gap or no separator, and give it one cue of its own, such as weight, the kicker green or an outline. Keep it quiet enough for the calm tone of the page.
