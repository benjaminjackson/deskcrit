**New findings**
1. **"Subscribe" is styled as one more section in the nav.** This is a convention issue. At the top right, "Subscribe" sits in the same dotted list as "Features · Food · Places", in the same gray, size, and weight. Features, Food, and Places are sections you go to read. Subscribe is the one thing the magazine wants a reader to do. Set the same way, it reads as a fourth content category, and a reader scanning the header has no cue that this is where they sign up. That's the action a small independent magazine most needs readers to find. The nav's grouping should show how its parts relate (Norman, *The Design of Everyday Things*, ch. 2, conceptual model). Fix: pull Subscribe out of the dotted list and give it a little space of its own. Give it one cue that sets it apart from the section links, such as the kicker's green or a light outline button. Keep it quieter than the headline.
