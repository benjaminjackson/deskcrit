**New findings**
1. **The headline and subline break in the wrong places (convention).** At the top left, the headline's first line ends on "with the" and the name "Wayfarer pack" starts the next line. The article ends up hanging at the end of line 1 and the product name is cut off from it, so the eye reads "Travel lighter with the" as an unfinished thought before it reaches the name. This is the biggest type on the page, so the break shows. The subline has the same problem at a smaller size: line 1 ends "opens flat like a" and "suitcase" drops to the next line, which separates the comparison from the word it hangs on. Headings and short text should break at natural phrase boundaries (Bringhurst, *The Elements of Typographic Style* 2.4, and Hochuli, *Detail in Typography*, "The line", who warns against single words left standing at the ends of lines).
   - **Fix:** set the breaks by hand. Break the headline as "Travel lighter / with the Wayfarer pack", or narrow the measure until it breaks there. In the subline, keep "a suitcase" together with a non-breaking space or a slightly different measure. If the headline gets rewritten, check its new break the same way.
