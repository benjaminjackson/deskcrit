**New findings**
1. **Four identical "Learn more" links.** All four cards end in the same blue "Learn more" link (around y 642–672). Someone scanning the links, or hearing them read out by a screen reader, gets four links with the same name and no hint of where each one goes. The text only makes sense next to its card. WCAG 2.4.4 (Link Purpose) asks that link text say where the link goes. The fix: name the destination in each link, for example "More on time zones" and "More on reminders", or make the card title the link. *Convention.*
2. **The card icons are drawn in two styles, and one doesn't match its feature.** In the icon row at about y 460–490, the "Shared calendars" calendar is an outline. The clock, envelope and layout icons are solid shapes. Side by side the set looks pieced together from different sources, which undercuts how tidy the row is meant to look. The "Intake forms" icon is also a generic page-layout grid, so it doesn't say "form" the way the other three say calendar, clock and mail. The fix: use one icon family in one style, all outline or all filled, and give intake forms a form or clipboard icon. *Taste.*
