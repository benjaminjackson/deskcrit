**New findings**
1. **There's no overall conversion rate anywhere on the page (convention).** The page only breaks the rate down by plan. The chart has four bars, and the "Trials by plan, Q3 2025" table at top right stops after Enterprise with no total row. So the figure a growth team asks for first, how many trials converted this quarter overall, isn't on the page. Readers have to add 18,420 trials and 627 paid accounts themselves. The plan mix makes this worse. Starter is half of all trials (9,210), so the overall rate sits close to Starter's 3.1%, not near the 3.6% you'd guess by eye from the middle of the four bars. The reader also can't see how much each plan contributes to the total. A dashboard's headline figure should be on the page, not left for the reader to work out (Few, "Common Pitfalls in Dashboard Design"). Fix: add a Total row to the table. Also show the overall rate as a single figure near the headline, or as a reference line across the chart, so readers can see each plan above or below the average.
