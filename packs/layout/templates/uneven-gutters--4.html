The Harbour Gazette

New quay opens

The long wall at the east end is finished and boats can tie up from Monday. The council thanked the crew for working through the storms.

Ferry timetable

From next month the ferry adds a late crossing on Fridays. Tickets can be bought on board or at the kiosk by the clock.

Market notes

Stalls on the square sell smoked fish, apples, wool socks and candles. The market runs until four, rain or shine.

Lifeboat day

The crew open the shed on Saturday with tours, a cake stall and a raffle for a hamper of local cheese.
