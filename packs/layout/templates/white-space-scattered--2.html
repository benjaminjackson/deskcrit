A short history of the village bell

By the parish archive team

The bell was cast in a field behind the church in the spring of 1788, when the old one cracked during a winter service. Villagers brought pots and pans to be melted into the new metal.

For a century it rang the hours, called the harvest workers in from the fields, and warned of floods when the river rose.

Its note was said to carry as far as the next valley.

The bell is rung by hand on the first Sunday of each month.

Visitors may climb the tower on open days. Ask the vicar for a place.
