Garden planner

Seed trays

Sow tomatoes and peppers indoors six weeks before the last frost.

Raised beds

Fill new beds with a mix of compost and topsoil in early spring.

Watering

Water deeply twice a week rather than a little every day.

Mulch

Spread straw between rows to keep the soil cool and moist.

Pruning

Pinch the side shoots off tomatoes once a week all summer.

Harvest

Pick beans every few days so the plants keep flowering.
