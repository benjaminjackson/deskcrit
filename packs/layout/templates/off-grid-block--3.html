A short guide to the tide tables

Tide tables list the time and height of each high and low water for one harbour. Read the harbour name first, because the same coast can differ by an hour from one bay to the next.

Times are printed in local time unless the header says otherwise. Heights are measured from the chart datum, the level below which the water rarely falls.

On a spring tide the range is largest, and the current in narrow channels runs fastest about two hours after the turn.

Plan a landing for the hour before high water and leave a margin for wind, which can hold the sea back or push it up the shore.
