Walks for every season

Six marked trails start from the village green. The shortest loop takes forty minutes, and the ridge path takes most of a day.

Each trail has benches at the best views, and a map is pinned to the gate at every trailhead so that no one sets out unsure of the way back.

Allotments to rent

Plots are let by the season and come with a water butt and a key to the tool shed.

Ask at the parish office for the waiting list. New tenants are offered a free packet of seed potatoes in their first spring, and a neighbour will happily show you where the soil is best.
