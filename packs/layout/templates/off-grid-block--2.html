Walking

Three days along the old drove road

By the editors, 12 minute read

The drove road leaves the village behind a row of cottages and climbs for an hour before the first view opens. By then the traffic has gone and the only sound is wind in the thorn hedges.

The first ridge, seen from the lower pasture.

Most walkers stop at the second gate, where a stone trough still collects rain. Those who carry on reach the tops by noon and have the whole plain laid out below them.
