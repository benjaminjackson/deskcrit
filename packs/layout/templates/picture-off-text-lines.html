The light at Gull Point

For more than a century the white tower at Gull Point has marked the mouth of the estuary, and the keepers who once climbed its ninety steps each evening are remembered by the brass plaques in the lamp room.

Today the lamp turns on a timer, but the tower is open to visitors on summer weekends. Climb to the gallery on a clear morning and you can follow the ferry all the way across the bay to the harbour wall.

Tickets are sold at the cottage by the car park, and the tea room beside it bakes scones every morning.
