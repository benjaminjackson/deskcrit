The ridge path in autumn

Seven miles of heather, stone walls, and one very good tea stop.

Start at the old mill car park and follow the stream uphill until the trees thin out.

The ridge opens on the right, with the whole valley laid out below like a patchwork.

Keep to the wall for a mile. The ground is soft after rain, so wear boots.

At the stone barn the path drops to the village, and the tea room opens at two.

Buses back to the mill run on the hour, and the last one leaves at six.
