Move your project to a new team

Transfers keep every file, comment, and share link.

1. Check your role

Only a team owner can start a transfer. Open Team settings and look for the owner badge beside your name.

2. Choose the new team

Pick the destination from the list. You must be a member of both teams to see it.

3. Confirm the transfer

Review the summary of files and members that will move, then type the project name to confirm.

4. Tell your collaborators

People with existing share links keep access. Members of the old team lose access unless you invite them.
