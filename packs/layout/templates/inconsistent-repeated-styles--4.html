How the old canal lock works

Filling the chamber

A keeper opens small paddles in the upper gates, and water flows in until the chamber level matches the higher pound.

Opening the gates

With the levels equal, the heavy oak gates swing open with a gentle push on the long balance beam.

Moving the boat

The boat is hauled in, the gates are closed behind it, and the lower paddles are drawn to let the water out.

Leaving below

When the chamber matches the lower pound, the far gates open and the boat carries on downstream.
