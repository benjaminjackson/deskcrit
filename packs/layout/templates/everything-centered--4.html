About the library

A reading room for the whole town

The Millbrook Library opened in a converted corn store and now lends more than nine thousand books a year. Volunteers run the Saturday story hour, the seed swap and the quiet reading evenings. Everyone is welcome, whether you come for a novel, a map or a warm place to sit.

The reading room is open six days a week, and the garden at the back is open for lunch in good weather.
