What to plant in the last warm weeks of autumn

By the Field Notes staff, October 24

Even after the first cool nights, the soil keeps its warmth for several weeks. That makes autumn a good time to put in garlic, spring bulbs, and hardy greens that settle in before winter.

Start by loosening the top few inches of soil and mixing in a thin layer of compost. Water well after planting, then cover the bed with straw to hold moisture and slow the first frosts.
