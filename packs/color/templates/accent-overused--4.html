Roasted tomato and white bean pasta

Prep 10 minCook 20 minServes 4

A pan of tomatoes roasted until they collapse makes the sauce, and a can of beans makes it a full meal.

Method

1Heat the oven to 425 degrees. Toss the tomatoes and garlic with oil on a sheet pan and roast 20 minutes.
2Boil the pasta in salted water until just tender. Save a cup of the water before draining.
3Stir the beans into the pan, crush the tomatoes, and toss with the pasta and a splash of the water.
Save recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 pound cherry tomatoes
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 can white beans, drained
  • 12 ounces short pasta
  • Olive oil and salt
