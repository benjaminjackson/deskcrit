How satisfied are the teams?

We asked every member of staff how satisfied they are with their work, on a scale from 0 to 10. 412 people answered, 86 percent of all staff.

Operations and Design gave the highest scores. Support gave the lowest, and its score fell 0.3 points since 2024.
