Research brief

How residents of Corvan Bay get to work

We asked 2,140 working residents of Corvan Bay one question in the spring 2026 household survey: How do you usually travel to work? Each person gave one answer, the mode they use on most days.

Driving alone is still the most common answer, but buses and bicycles together now account for more trips than it does. Walking is strongest in the old harbour wards, where most jobs sit within two kilometres of home.

Method: online and phone panel, 3 to 28 April 2026, weighted to ward population. Margin of error ±2 points.
