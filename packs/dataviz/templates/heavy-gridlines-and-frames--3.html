Library visits by branch, 2025

Thousands of visits counted at the door, January to December

Source: Tarnley City Libraries door counters. Mobile library stops are not included.

About this dataset

Each branch counts visitors with a sensor at the main entrance. Counts are summed by calendar year.

Central library includes the reference floor and the children's wing.
