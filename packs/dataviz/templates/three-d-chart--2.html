Station access survey 2026 · Section 2

How riders reach Kestrel Valley station

Main way of getting to the station on the day of the survey, share of riders, May 2026.

Share of riders by way of reaching the station

  • Walked38%
  • Bus27%
  • Car, dropped off17%
  • Bicycle11%
  • Car, parked7%

Walking is the most common way to reach the station. The new bus link from the east estate, opened in January, now brings more than a quarter of riders.

Source: Kestrel Valley Rail Partnership platform survey, 6 to 10 May 2026, 1,214 riders.
