Policy brief No. 14 · Ashgrove Institute for Urban Health

Longer commutes go with less weekly exercise

Key findings

  • Districts with a median commute under 20 minutes report about 6 hours of exercise a week.
  • Above 60 minutes, the median falls to about 3 hours.
  • The link holds after we control for age and household income.
  • Districts with protected cycle routes sit above the trend at every commute length.
