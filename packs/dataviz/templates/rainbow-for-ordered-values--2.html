Households per hectare in each of the county's 29 districts. Source: Harrowmere County Planning Office, household register, March.

Homes cluster around the old ferry crossing

The districts nearest the river crossing hold the most households per hectare, and the count falls steadily toward the hill farms at the county edge.

Three districts on the east bank passed 36 households per hectare for the first time this year, after the Millrace flats opened in the spring.
