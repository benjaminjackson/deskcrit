Section 3

Rainfall at the upland and lowland gauges

The upland gauge at Hollin Ridge recorded 1,212 mm over the year, about 60 percent more than the lowland gauge at Marsh End.

Both gauges followed the same seasonal pattern. Totals fell from January to a low in July and rose again through the autumn.

December was the wettest month at both sites, which matches the long-run record for the basin.
