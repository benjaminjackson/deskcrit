Climate notes

A warm July, and a typical year on the ridge

The station's thirty-year record puts the warmest month in July, when the average afternoon high reaches 23 degrees.

Winters stay mild for the altitude. January and December both average a high near 4 degrees, and frost days have fallen by a week since the record began.

Spring warms faster than autumn cools: the rise from March to May is steeper than the fall from September to November.
