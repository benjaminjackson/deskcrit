Changing your pickup time

Open your order and choose a new slot from the list. The bakery holds orders for two hours after the chosen time.

Collecting for someone else

Give the person your order number. They will need to show it at the counter along with a name.

Allergies and ingredients

Every loaf has a card by the till listing what is in it. Ask the baker if you are unsure about anything.

Cancelling an order

Orders can be cancelled up to a day ahead. After that the bread has already been baked for you.
