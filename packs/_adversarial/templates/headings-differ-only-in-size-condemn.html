Planning a vegetable bed

Choose a spot that gets six hours of sun and stays out of the wind. Mark the corners with sticks and a length of string.

Preparing the soil

Dig to the depth of a spade and mix in compost from last year's heap. Rake the surface level and leave it to settle for a week.

Choosing what to plant

Start with things that forgive mistakes: radishes, lettuce, and bush beans. Sow in short rows so each kind is easy to find.

Watering

Water deeply in the morning, twice a week, rather than a little every day.
