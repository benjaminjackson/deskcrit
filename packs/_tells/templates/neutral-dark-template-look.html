Know when the water is slack before you leave the car

Slackwater shows the tide, the current, and the water temperature for the beaches you swim from, hour by hour. It marks the safe window for each swim and tells your group when it moves.

From the car park to the water

  1. Pick your beaches

    Add the beaches you swim from. Slackwater reads the tide tables and the buoys nearest each one.

  2. See the safe window

    Each day shows when the current is weakest, and how warm the water was at the last reading.

  3. Swim with your group

    Share a beach with your group, and everyone gets a message when the window moves.
