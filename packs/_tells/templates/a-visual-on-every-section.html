Every shelf of the kiln booked before the firing

Kilnroom lets the members of a pottery studio book space on the next bisque or glaze firing, shelf by shelf. The technician sees what is going in, what cone it needs, and who to tell when it comes out.

From the wheel to the shelf

  1. Book a shelf

    Members pick a firing and a shelf height from their phone, and see what else is on it.

  2. Label the work

    Each booking gets a number to write on the foot, so pieces find their owner afterwards.

  3. Collect when told

    When the kiln is cool, everyone with work in it gets a message to come and collect.

For studios that teach

Book a whole shelf for a class in one go and name the students later. Beginners see when their first bowls come out without asking the teacher.

  • Class bookings that hold space until the week of the firing
  • A shared shelf for pieces with no name yet
  • A list for the teacher of who has collected
