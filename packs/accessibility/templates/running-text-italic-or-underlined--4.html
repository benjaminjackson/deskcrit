Getting ready for your blood test

Most blood tests need no preparation. Your appointment letter says whether yours is one of the few that do.

If your letter says the test is a fasting test, do not eat or drink anything except water for 10 hours before your appointment. Keep taking your usual medicines unless your doctor has told you to stop, and bring a list of them with you.

Drink a glass of water an hour before you come in. It makes the vein easier to find, and the test is usually over in five minutes.

Need to change the time?

Call us before 10 am on the day, or change it online up to two hours before.
