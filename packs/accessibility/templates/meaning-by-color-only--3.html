Baking basics

A rye loaf you can start after work

This loaf needs about twenty minutes of hands-on time, spread across one evening and the next morning. If you have never kept a starter, begin with our guide to a rye starter a week before you bake.

Rye flour holds more water than wheat, so the dough will feel sticky and it will not rise into a dome. That is normal. Wet your hands instead of adding flour, and use a tin rather than a tray. The tin we use is listed in our kitchen kit.

Bake it until the crust is dark and the base sounds hollow, then leave it wrapped in a cloth for a full day. Rye cut while warm turns gummy, as we explain in why rye needs to rest.
