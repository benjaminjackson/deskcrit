Prices are in US dollars and do not include sales tax. On a monthly plan you pay for the number of people in the workspace on the day the invoice is made, so adding someone mid-month costs nothing until the next invoice. Yearly plans are paid in advance, and people added during the year are billed for the months that remain. You can move between plans at any time from the billing page.
