Two Rivers Ride 2026

Three routes leave the market square between 8 and 10 am: a 20 km family loop, a 60 km ride along both rivers, and a 110 km route over the hills. Every route ends back in the square, where the food stalls open at noon.

Bring a helmet, a working rear light, a spare inner tube, and enough water for the first hour. There are refill points every 15 km on the longer routes, and the mechanics at each point can fix a puncture but cannot replace a broken chain.
