Billing / Payment methods

Update the card that pays for your subscription

You can replace the card on file at any time from the billing page. The new card is charged from the next invoice, and any invoice that is already open keeps the old card until it is paid. If you are not sure which invoice is next, check your billing history before you make the change.

Only an account owner or a member with the billing role can change the card. To give someone that role, see roles and permissions. If your company pays by bank transfer instead of by card, follow the steps in paying by invoice instead.

When the new card is declined

We try a declined card again after three days and after seven days, and we send an email each time. Your workspace stays open during this period. If the card is still declined after the second attempt, the subscription moves to the free plan, and you can restore your previous plan as soon as a working card is added.
