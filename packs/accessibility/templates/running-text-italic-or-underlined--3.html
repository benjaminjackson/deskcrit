Brass lantern from a river ferry

About 1880 · Brass and glass

This lantern hung at the bow of a small passenger ferry for more than forty years. The dents on its left side match the post at the old landing stage, where the ferry tied up eight times a day. The glass was replaced twice, but the frame and the handle are the originals.
