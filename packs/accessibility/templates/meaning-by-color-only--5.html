Bin collection days

Collections over the winter holidays move by one day. Put your bins out by 7 am on the new day.

Find your day

Your collection day depends on your street. Enter your postcode on the collection day finder to see the next three dates for each bin. You can also sign up for a reminder by text message the evening before.

If your bin was not emptied on the right day, leave it out and report a missed collection within two working days.

What goes in each bin

The green bin takes garden waste only. Food waste goes in the small brown caddy, lined with paper or with the bags that our libraries hand out free of charge.

Glass, cans, and clean plastic go together in the blue bin. For large items such as furniture, book a bulky waste collection instead.
