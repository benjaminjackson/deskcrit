The allotment at the end of the bus line

By Theo Marchetti · Photographs by the residents

Ten years ago the plot behind the last stop was a car park nobody used. Today forty households grow beans, squash, and herbs there, and on Saturday mornings the queue for the shared water tap is the best place in the district to hear the news. We spent a season with the people who built it.

It started with a letter. Halima Osei, who has lived on the estate since 1998, wrote to the transport authority to ask whether the empty car park could be lent to residents for one summer. The reply took four months and said yes, on the condition that nothing was built that could not be moved.

So the first beds were pallets and old bath tubs. Most of them are still in use.
