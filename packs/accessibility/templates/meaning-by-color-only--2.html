The night ferry returns, but only on weekends for now

By Mara Ellison · 9 min read

After two years without a late service, the crossing to the north shore will run again from next month. The operator says the boats will leave every forty minutes until one in the morning on Fridays and Saturdays, a timetable it first described in last spring's service review.

Residents of the island villages have asked for the service since it was cut. A petition gathered more than four thousand names, and the council debated it twice, most recently at the October transport meeting. Councillors agreed to pay for a one-year trial on the condition that ridership is published each quarter.

Fares will match the daytime fares. Monthly passes will be valid on the late boats, and the operator plans to add the new times to its app before the first sailing. A full list of stops is on the operator's route page.
