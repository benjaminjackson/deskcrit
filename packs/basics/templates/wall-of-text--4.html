Saturday bread from the Corner Oven

The Corner Oven bakes its bread in small batches every night and opens the shop door at seven, when the first loaves are still warm from the oven.

The sourdough takes two full days to make, because the dough rests slowly in the cold room before it is shaped, and that patience gives the crust its deep colour and the crumb its open, chewy texture.

Sourdough

The plain loaf rests for two days in the cold room before it is baked.

  • Collect your loaf before noon.
  • Bring a paper bag or a cloth.
  • Tell us two days before a holiday.

Rye

Subscribers collect a loaf every Saturday morning and may choose between the plain sourdough, the rye with caraway, and the seeded wholemeal.

A subscription can be paused for holidays with two days of notice, and any loaf you miss is given to the food bank at the end of the day, so nothing goes to waste in the shop.
