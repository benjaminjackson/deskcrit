Print a return label

Every return needs a label with a barcode, so the drop-off point knows where the parcel goes. You can print the label at home or show a code on your phone at the counter and let the staff print it for you.

To print at home:

  1. Open your order and choose the items you are sending back.
  2. Pick a reason for each item, then choose a drop-off point near you.
  3. Print the label on plain paper and tape it flat to the largest side of the box.

Cover any old labels or barcodes on the box, because a scanner can read the wrong one and send the parcel back to you. Keep the receipt from the drop-off point until your refund arrives.
