Seasonal bouquets

From £35

Each week we pick the best flowers from local growers and arrange them by hand in the shop. In spring that means tulips, ranunculus, and blossom branches, and later in the year it means dahlias, roses, and dried grasses.

Bouquets are wrapped in recycled paper and come with a card for your message. Order before noon for delivery the same day anywhere in town, or collect your flowers from the shop after two o'clock.

Because the flowers change with the season, your bouquet may look different from the one in the photograph, but it will always be the same size and value.
