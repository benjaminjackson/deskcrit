Plots at the Elm Court community garden

Plots at the Elm Court community garden are let for the growing season, from March to October, and each plot measures about four metres by two.

Gardeners share the water barrels, the tool shed, and the compost bins at the north end, and everyone is asked to leave the paths clear of weeds, hoses, and wheelbarrows so that neighbours can pass safely with their baskets.

Watering

Use the barrels first and the tap only when they are empty, ideally early in the morning.

  • Sign in on the board by the gate.
  • Return every tool to its hook.
  • Close and latch the gate when you leave.

Compost

New gardeners are paired with an experienced neighbour for their first month, who can explain which crops do well in the heavy clay soil near the wall and which prefer the raised beds beside the gate.

Seeds are swapped on the first Sunday of each month, and anyone is welcome to bring a spare packet or a tray of seedlings to the table.
