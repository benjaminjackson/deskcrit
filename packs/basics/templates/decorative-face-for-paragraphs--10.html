Riverside summer concerts

Free music on the lawn every Thursday in July

Bring a blanket and a picnic to the riverside lawn for an evening of live music. Each concert starts at seven and finishes before nine, with a short break in the middle, and the programme changes every week, from brass bands to folk and jazz.

The lawn opens at six, and the food stalls by the boathouse sell drinks, ice cream, and hot food until the end of the evening. Seating is not provided, so please bring your own chair or blanket.

If it rains heavily, the concert moves into the town hall on Bridge Street. We post any change on this page by four o'clock on the day.
