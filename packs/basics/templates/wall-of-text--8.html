How we moved our nightly backups

For three years our nightly backups ran on a single server in the office cupboard, which copied every database to a pair of external disks.

It worked, but a failed disk or a missed Friday left a gap that nobody noticed until we needed the files.

What we changed

This spring we moved the backups to object storage in two regions. Each night a small script now does three things:

  • copies every database to both regions;
  • checks each copy against the one from the night before;
  • deletes copies that are older than ninety days.

What we learned

The move took about six weeks, and most of that time went on testing restores, not on the copying itself.

A short report now lands in our team chat each morning and lists any backup that did not finish.
