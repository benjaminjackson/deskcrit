Getting married at Thornbury Barn

Thornbury Barn sits at the end of a quiet lane, with an orchard on one side and open fields on the other. The main barn seats up to one hundred and twenty guests for dinner, and the old dairy next door makes a warm room for drinks and speeches.

We host one wedding each day, so the barn, the orchard, and the gardens are yours from ten in the morning until midnight. Our coordinator meets you twice before the day to plan the timings, the tables, and the music.

Visits are on Saturday mornings by appointment. Write to us with the dates you have in mind and we will send our prices.
