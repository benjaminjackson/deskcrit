Riverside summer concerts

Free music on the lawn every Thursday in July. Each concert starts at seven and finishes before nine, and the programme changes every week, from brass bands to folk and jazz.

What to bring

The lawn opens at six, and the food stalls by the boathouse sell drinks, ice cream, and hot food until the end of the evening. Seating is not provided, so please bring your own chair or blanket and leave the paths clear for others.

If it rains

If it rains heavily, the concert moves into the town hall on Bridge Street. We post any change on this page by four o'clock on the day.
