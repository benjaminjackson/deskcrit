Walking the Pine Ridge trail

The Pine Ridge trail climbs steadily for six kilometres from the quarry gate to the lookout, and most walkers need about three hours to reach the top at an easy pace.

The path is well marked with yellow paint on the trunks, but after rain the lower section becomes muddy and slippery, so sturdy boots with a good grip are strongly recommended.

Footwear

Wear sturdy boots with a good grip, because the lower path is muddy after rain.

  • Tell someone your route before you go.
  • Carry water, a hat, and a warm layer.
  • Turn back if the mist comes down.

Water

Water is not available on the ridge, so carry at least a litre for each person and more in warm weather.

The only shelter is a small timber hut about halfway up, which has a bench and a roof but no walls on the windward side, and it makes a pleasant place to rest and eat lunch before the final climb.
