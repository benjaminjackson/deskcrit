Notes for ferry passengers

The river ferry runs from the north landing to the market quay every forty minutes between early morning and late evening.

Each crossing takes about twelve minutes in calm weather, and a little longer when the tide turns against the boat.

Boarding

Foot passengers board first, from the ramp on the upstream side.

Riders with bicycles wait at the gate until the crew waves them on, then wheel the bicycles to the lower deck.

  • Lock bicycles to the rail along the side of the deck.
  • Keep bags on the bicycle during the crossing.
  • Leave the stairs clear for people walking up to the top deck.

Tickets

Single and return tickets are sold on board and at the landing kiosk.

A season pass covers any number of crossings for a month, and the crew checks it as you board.

Winter service

In winter the timetable thins to one boat each hour, and the last crossing leaves the quay before dusk.

Printed timetables are kept in the shelter at each landing, and the noticeboard there lists any changes for the coming week.
