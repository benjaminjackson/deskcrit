Opening hours at the Maple Street library

The Maple Street library opens at nine on weekdays and stays open until eight in the evening, with a shorter day on Saturdays when the children's room hosts a reading hour for families.

Borrowers may keep up to twelve books for three weeks, and any title can be renewed once by telephone or at the front desk, unless another reader is waiting for it.

Borrowing

Each reader may keep up to twelve books for three weeks, and renew a title once by telephone.

  • Bring your library card to the front desk.
  • Return books through the slot beside the door.
  • Ask a volunteer when a title is missing.

Reading room

The reading room on the first floor is kept quiet, and the long tables by the windows fill up quickly after school.

Study booths can be reserved for two hours at a time, and the staff will gladly help you find a free one, or suggest a corner of the garden court when every booth is taken during the busy weeks before exams.
