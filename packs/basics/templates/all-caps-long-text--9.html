Before your first appointment

Please arrive ten minutes before your appointment so that we have time to check your details and answer any questions.

Your first visit usually takes about forty minutes. The dentist will look at your teeth and gums, take X-rays if they are needed, and talk you through any treatment they recommend before anything is done.

Please note

If you are pregnant, take blood thinners, or have a heart condition, tell the dentist before your check-up begins, because it may change the treatment we can safely offer you.
