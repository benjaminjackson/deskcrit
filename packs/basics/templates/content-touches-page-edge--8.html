How we moved our nightly backups

For three years our nightly backups ran on a single server in the office cupboard. It copied every database to a pair of external disks, and someone took one of the disks home each Friday.

This spring we moved the backups to object storage in two regions. Each database is now copied every night, checked against the previous copy, and kept for ninety days.
