How the river ferry runs in winter

From November to March the ferry leaves the north landing every forty minutes. The first crossing is at seven in the morning and the last at six in the evening.

When ice forms near the banks, the crew may hold the boat at the landing until the channel is clear. Check the board at the ticket hut before you queue.

Bicycles ride free on weekdays. Cars pay the standard fare, and vans over two metres high use the cargo deck at the stern.
