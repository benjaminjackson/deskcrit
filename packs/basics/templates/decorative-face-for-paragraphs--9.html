Birthday parties at the Splash Pool

For children aged five to twelve, on Saturday and Sunday afternoons.

In the water

An hour in the small pool with floats, slides, and games, led by two of our lifeguards. Every child who cannot swim yet wears arm bands, and parents can watch from the gallery.

Party room

After the swim, the party moves to our room by the café for forty-five minutes. We lay the tables and serve sandwiches, fruit, and juice, and you are welcome to bring your own cake.

Booking

Parties are for up to sixteen children and cost £180. Book at the front desk or by phone at least two weeks ahead, and pay a deposit of £50 to hold the date.
