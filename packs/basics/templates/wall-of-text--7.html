Stoneware pour-over set

This pour-over set is thrown by hand from a speckled stoneware clay and finished in a soft oat glaze.

In the box

  • A dripper that holds a size 2 paper filter.
  • A carafe that pours up to four cups.

Care

Every piece is fired twice and is safe in the dishwasher and the microwave. Do not put the carafe on a hob or an open flame.

Because each set is made by hand, the glaze and the size can vary a little from the photographs, and no two sets are exactly the same.
