Baked through the night, sold by mid-morning

We start the ovens at three every morning, so the sourdough, rye, and seeded loaves are on the shelves when the door opens at seven. Most of the bread is gone by eleven, and on Saturdays it often goes sooner.

If you want a particular loaf, order it the day before and we will keep it behind the counter with your name on it. Celebration cakes need three days' notice, and we are glad to talk through flavours and sizes over the phone.
