Before your first appointment

Please arrive ten minutes before your appointment so that we have time to check your details and answer any questions. If you take any medicines, bring a list of their names and doses, because some of them can affect your treatment.

Your first visit usually takes about forty minutes. The dentist will look at your teeth and gums, take X-rays if they are needed, and talk you through any treatment they recommend before anything is done.

If you need to change your appointment, call the front desk at least one working day before your visit and we will find you a new time that suits you.
