Before your first appointment

Please arrive ten minutes before your appointment so that we have time to check your details and answer any questions.

What to bring

  • A list of any medicines you take, with their doses.
  • Your last dental X-rays, if you have them.
  • Glasses, if you need them to read a form.

During the visit

Your first visit usually takes about forty minutes. The dentist will look at your teeth and gums and take X-rays if they are needed.

Before any treatment is done, the dentist talks you through what they recommend and what it costs, and you can take the plan home to think it over.

Children under sixteen must come with a parent or carer.
