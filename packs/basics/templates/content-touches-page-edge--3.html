The night the lamp went out

The lamp on the point had burned every night for sixty years, and nobody on the island remembered it ever failing. So when the light died a little after nine, the whole village came out to look.

The keeper climbed the stairs with a torch in his teeth. At the top he found the wick drowned in oil, because a gull had knocked the cap off the tank.

By ten the glass was clean, the wick was dry, and the beam swept across the water again. The children were sent to bed, but most of them watched from the windows.
