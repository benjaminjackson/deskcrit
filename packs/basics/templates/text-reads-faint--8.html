How we moved our nightly backups

For three years our nightly backups ran on a single server in the office cupboard. It copied every database to a pair of external disks, and someone took one of the disks home each Friday. It worked, but a failed disk or a missed Friday left a gap that nobody noticed until we needed the files.

This spring we moved the backups to object storage in two regions. Each database is now copied every night, checked against the previous copy, and kept for ninety days. A short report lands in our team chat each morning and lists any backup that did not finish.

The move took about six weeks, most of it spent testing restores. Below we describe the scripts we wrote and what the new setup costs each month.
