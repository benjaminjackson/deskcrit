Thursdays in July

Riverside summer concerts

Bring a blanket and a picnic to the riverside lawn for an evening of free live music. Each concert starts at seven and finishes before nine.

This year's programme

  • 3 July: the Millbrook Brass Band.
  • 10 July: folk songs with the Weir Street Trio.
  • 17 July: the town youth orchestra.
  • 24 and 31 July: jazz on the lawn.

Food and seating

The lawn opens at six, and the food stalls by the boathouse sell drinks, ice cream, and hot food until the end of the evening.

Seating is not provided, so please bring your own chair or blanket and leave the paths clear for others.

If it rains heavily, the concert moves into the town hall on Bridge Street. We post any change on this page by four o'clock on the day.
