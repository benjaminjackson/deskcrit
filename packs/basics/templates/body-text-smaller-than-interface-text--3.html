Ingredients

  • 2 leeks, washed and sliced
  • 500 g floury potatoes
  • 1 litre vegetable stock
  • 30 g butter
  • A handful of chives
  • Salt and black pepper

Leek and potato soup

Save recipe Print

Method

Melt the butter in a large pan over a low heat. Add the leeks with a pinch of salt, cover, and let them soften for ten minutes, stirring now and then so they do not brown.

Peel the potatoes and cut them into small cubes. Add them to the pan with the stock, bring it to a gentle simmer, and cook for twenty minutes, until a cube breaks when you press it against the side of the pan.

Blend half of the soup until smooth and stir it back into the rest, so the soup is thick but still has pieces in it. Season to taste and serve with the chives cut over the top.
