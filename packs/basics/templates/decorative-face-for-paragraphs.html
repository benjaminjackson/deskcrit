Notes for ferry passengers

The river ferry runs from the north landing to the market quay every forty minutes between early morning and late evening. Each crossing takes about twelve minutes in calm weather, and a little longer when the tide turns against the boat.

Bicycles ride on the lower deck at no extra charge. Riders wheel them on after foot passengers have boarded, and lock them to the rail along the side of the deck.

In winter the timetable thins to one boat each hour, and the last crossing leaves the quay before dusk. Printed timetables are kept in the shelter at each landing.
