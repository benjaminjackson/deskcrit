Stoneware pour-over set

This pour-over set is thrown by hand from a speckled stoneware clay and finished in a soft oat glaze. It includes a dripper that holds a standard size 2 paper filter and a matching carafe that pours up to four cups at a time, and both pieces stack neatly on the shelf.

Every piece is fired twice and is safe in the dishwasher and the microwave. Because each set is made by hand, the glaze and the size can vary a little from the photographs, and no two sets are exactly the same as each other.
