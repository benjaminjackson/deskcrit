Walking the Pine Ridge trail

Dogs are welcome on the trail but must stay on a lead near the farm fields, where lambs graze in the spring, and owners are asked to carry out anything their dog leaves behind on the path.

The car park at the quarry gate fills early on fine weekends, so arrive before nine if you can, or walk up from the village along the old railway path, which adds about an hour.

Mobile signal is weak on the north side of the ridge, so tell someone your route before you set out and agree a time by which you expect to be back home.

Wear sturdy boots with a good grip, because the lower path is muddy after rain. There is no water on the ridge, so carry a litre for each person in your group.
