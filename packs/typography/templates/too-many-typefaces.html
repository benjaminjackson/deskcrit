Crossing the river

A short guide for passengers on the north landing to market quay route.

When the boats run

The ferry leaves every forty minutes between early morning and late evening. In winter the timetable thins to one boat each hour, and the last crossing leaves before dusk.

Bicycles and luggage

Bicycles ride on the lower deck at no extra charge. Riders wheel them on after foot passengers have boarded, and lock them to the rail along the side of the deck.

Good to know

Printed timetables are kept in the shelter at each landing, and the noticeboard lists any changes for the coming week.
