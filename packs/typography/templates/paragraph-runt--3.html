Plots at the Elm Court community garden

Plots at the Elm Court community garden are let for the growing season, from March to October, and each plot measures about four metres by two. Gardeners share the water barrels, the tool shed, and the compost bins at the north end, and everyone is asked to leave the paths clear of weeds, hoses, and wheelbarrows so that neighbours can pass safely with their baskets.

The tool shed is unlocked each morning by whoever arrives first, and the last gardener to leave checks that every spade, fork, and hose is back on its hook before the gate is closed for the night.

Compost bins are for kitchen scraps, leaves, and soft garden waste only, so please keep plastic, glass, and woody branches out of them and carry anything else home to the bin.
