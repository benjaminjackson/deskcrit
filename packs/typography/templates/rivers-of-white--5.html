Opening hours at the Maple Street library

We are in on the day at nine, and we go on to eight at night. On a Sat, it is a bit less, as we do a hour of tales for the kids in the big room at the back. You may take up to a dozen of the books for up to a few weeks, and if you do not want to let it go, you may ask for it to be put on for an act of one more go by the desk or on the phone, if no one else is on the list to get it.

We ask all of you to be as hush as a mouse in the big room on the top of the stair, as it is so for all of us to do our work in it. If you go for a booth, we can let you have it for two hours or so, and if no one is in it we may let you do a bit more, so ask us at the desk, or go out to sit on a bench in the yard if all of them are in use by the end of the day.
