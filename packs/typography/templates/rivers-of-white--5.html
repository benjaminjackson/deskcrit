Newsletter · Autumn 2027

Water troughs

The troughs on the upper path will be drained at the end of October, before the first frost can crack them. Please take what you need before then, and use the butts by the shed through the winter. If your own butt is overflowing, ask a neighbour whether they would like the spare water, because the lower plots dry out quickly in a cold, windy spell. The troughs will be filled again in March, once the committee has checked the pipes for leaks.

Seed swap

Our seed swap is on the first Sunday of February in the hut. Bring any seed you saved this year in a labelled envelope, with the name of the variety and the year it was collected, and take home something new to try.
