Pine Ridge trail

What to carry on the ridge walk

Six kilometres, one steady climb, and a view that is worth the effort on a clear day.

Packing list

  • Pack a waterproof jacket that fits over a jumper, because the weather on the ridge can turn from sun to sleet within an hour.
  • Carry at least one litre of water for each person and a small flask of something hot for the long rest at the timber hut.
  • Wear boots with a good grip, and keep a spare pair of dry socks in a plastic bag at the bottom of your pack.
  • Take a paper map and a torch even on a short day, and tell someone at home which route you plan to walk.
