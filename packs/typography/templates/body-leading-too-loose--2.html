Community garden

Plots at Elm Court

Plots at the Elm Court community garden are let for the growing season, from March to October, and each plot measures about four metres by two. Gardeners share the water barrels and the tool shed.

New gardeners are paired with an experienced neighbour for their first month. Seeds are swapped on the first Sunday of each month, and anyone is welcome to bring a spare packet or a tray of seedlings.
