Parish newsletter

November 2027

Lights for the green

The lights on the green will be switched on at five o'clock on the last Saturday of the month, with carols from the school choir and hot drinks from the cricket club.

Thank you to everyone who gave to the appeal. We raised enough to replace the old cables and to add a string of lights along the path to the church.

Volunteers are needed on the Friday afternoon to help hang the lights. Please bring gloves, and meet by the bench at the top of the green at two.
