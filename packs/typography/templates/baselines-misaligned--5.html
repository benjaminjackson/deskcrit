The Maple Street Library News

New reading room

The library's new reading room opens on the first of the month, with a long oak table, a low shelf of picture books, and a window seat that looks out over the garden court. Children are welcome until six, and a volunteer reads aloud each Thursday at four. Bring a cushion if you like to sit on the floor, and a friend if you like to read with company, because the room is quiet but not silent.

Winter lending list

Our winter lending list has grown by two hundred titles. Cookery and gardening books came first, followed by a shelf of local history and a box of large-print mysteries that have already been borrowed twice. Readers may place a hold on any title by telephone or at the desk, and the staff will send a card when the book is ready. Holds are kept for seven days on the shelf behind the counter.

Evening classes

Evening classes begin next week in the basement room, which has been repainted and fitted with proper lamps. Choose from beginner watercolour on Mondays, family history on Wednesdays, and a relaxed conversation circle for new neighbours on Fridays. Each class runs for six weeks, tea and biscuits are provided, and there is no charge for members. Spaces are limited, so please put your name on the sheet at the front desk.
