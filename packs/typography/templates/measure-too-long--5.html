Opening hours at the Maple Street library

The Maple Street library opens at nine on weekdays and stays open until eight in the evening, with a shorter day on Saturdays when the children's room hosts a reading hour for families. Borrowers may keep up to twelve books for three weeks, and any title can be renewed once by telephone or at the front desk, unless another reader is waiting for it.

The reading room on the first floor is kept quiet, and the long tables by the windows fill up quickly after school. Study booths can be reserved for two hours at a time, and the staff will gladly help you find a free one, or suggest a corner of the garden court when every booth is taken during the busy weeks before exams.

Photocopying costs a few coins a page, and the machine accepts exact change only. Please ask at the desk if you need a larger print or a copy of an old newspaper from the local archive, which is stored in the basement and brought upstairs by a volunteer within about an hour of your request.
