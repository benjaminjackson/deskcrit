Membership

What your membership includes

Your membership helps pay for the gardeners who look after the borders, the glasshouses, and the old walled orchard. In return you can visit as often as you like, bring a guest four times a year, and book talks and courses before they open to everyone else. Members also receive a printed magazine each season with news from the garden, a planting calendar, and notes from the head gardener on what to look out for on your next visit.
