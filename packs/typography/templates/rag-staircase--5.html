Field notes, no. 12

Counting swifts

Every evening in June I sit on the low wall by the church and count the swifts that come screaming round the tower. Some nights there are only a handful, high up and hard to follow. On warm, still evenings there can be forty or more, dropping so low over the roofs that you hear their wings. I write the numbers in a small notebook, with the time and the weather, and send them to the county bird group at the end of the month.
