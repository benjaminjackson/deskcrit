Pine Ridge

Walking the ridge

Six kilometres. About three hours.

The trail climbs steadily from the quarry gate to the lookout. The path is marked with yellow paint on the trunks, but after rain the lower section becomes muddy and slippery.

Water is not available on the ridge, so carry at least a litre for each person. The only shelter is a small timber hut about halfway up.

Mist can settle on the summit without warning, and the path across the open rock is easy to lose.
