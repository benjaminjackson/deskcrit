Notes from the boat shed

The shed sits at the end of the lane, where the tide comes up under the slipway twice a day. Inside, three clinker dinghies wait on trestles for their spring coat of varnish. The smell of tar and cut cedar hangs in the air all year.

Volunteers meet on Thursday evenings to scrape, sand, and paint. Nobody needs experience. There is always a kettle on, and a spare apron on the hook by the door for anyone who wants to help.
