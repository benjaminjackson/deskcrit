Ferry notes

Crossing the river by the daylight timetable

The river ferry leaves the north landing every forty minutes from seven in the morning until nine at night. Each crossing takes about twelve minutes, and the boat waits at the quay for five minutes so that passengers can step off and new ones can step on. Foot passengers board first, then bicycles, and the crew asks everyone to keep the gangway clear while the ramp is lowered. Tickets are sold at the kiosk on each landing, and a book of ten crossings costs less than ten single tickets.

In winter the timetable thins to one boat each hour, and the last crossing leaves the quay before dusk. When the river runs high after heavy rain the ferry may sail more slowly, and a crossing can take as long as twenty minutes. Notices about changes are pinned to the board in the shelter at each landing, and the crew will gladly answer questions about the next sailing. Dogs are welcome on the lower deck, and wheelchairs and prams roll straight on from the new ramps.
