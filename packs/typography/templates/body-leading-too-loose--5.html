The last boat home

A short story from the harbour

When the evening ferry slid away from the market quay, Marta stood at the rail and watched the lamps come on along the water. The crossing was short, and she knew every light by its colour.

By the time the boat reached the north landing the sky had turned the colour of slate, and the first stars showed over the mill. She carried her basket up the ramp and did not look back.
