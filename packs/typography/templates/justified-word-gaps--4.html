Reserving a room

Unfortunately, accommodation for extraordinarily large gatherings is unavailable during examination weeks, although straightforward arrangements can nevertheless be negotiated with the administrative office, provided representatives confirm approximately two days beforehand.

Photographers, musicians, and neighbourhood organisations should collect identification badges immediately upon arrival, and refreshments are permitted exclusively inside the community kitchen.
