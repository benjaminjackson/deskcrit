Three walks near the village

The northern loop

The northern loop starts at the car park by the quarry and climbs gently through pine woods for about three kilometres. The path is wide and dry, and it suits walkers of any age.

At the top there is a bench with a view over the valley, and most people stop here for a drink.

The way down follows a stream and brings you back to the start in under two hours.

The river path

The river path is flat and easy, and runs for five kilometres between the village and the old bridge. Herons often stand in the shallows, and in spring the banks are full of wild garlic.

Benches are placed every kilometre, each with a small plaque.

Cyclists share the path, so keep to the left and listen for bells.

The ridge

The ridge walk is longer and steeper, and is best left for a clear day. It takes about five hours, and there is no water on the way, so carry plenty.

From the highest point you can see the coast on one side and the mountains on the other.

Allow time to turn back if the weather changes.
