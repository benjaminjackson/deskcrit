Ashby District Council

Bin collections over the holidays

Collections move by one or two days around the bank holidays. Put your bins out by seven in the morning on the new day.

Christmas week

Collections due on Thursday 25 December happen on Saturday 27 December. Friday collections move to Monday 29 December.

New Year

Collections due on Thursday 1 January happen on Friday 2 January. All other days stay the same.

Christmas trees

Real trees can go in the garden waste bin in January if they are cut to fit with the lid closed. Remove all lights, pots, and stands first.
