Opening hours at the Maple Street library

Each reader may keep up to twelve books for three weeks, and renew a title once by telephone.

Reading room

The first floor is kept quiet from nine until closing, with long tables beside the tall windows.

Study booths

Reserve a booth for two hours at the front desk, or ask a volunteer to find you a free corner.

Local archive

Old newspapers are kept in the basement and brought up on request within about an hour.
