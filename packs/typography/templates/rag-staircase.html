About the trust

The Moorside Trust looks after eleven miles of footpath, two small woods, and the old quarry above the village. It was set up in 1987 by a group of walkers who wanted the paths kept open after the estate was sold. Today it has four staff, around ninety volunteers, and a membership of just over two thousand people, most of whom live within a few miles of the moor. Work days run every second Saturday, and anyone is welcome to join for the morning. We provide tools, gloves, and a hot drink at the end, and nobody needs any experience to help. Most of the work is clearing drains, mending stiles, and cutting back gorse where it has grown across the path.
