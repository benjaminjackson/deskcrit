Fit and fabric

The Tern rain jacket is cut long in the back and short in the sleeve, so it keeps the wind out on a bicycle and the rain off a notebook. The shell is a light woven fabric with taped seams, and the hood folds into the collar when it is not needed. Two deep pockets sit at the hip, and a zip pocket inside the chest holds a phone, a ticket, and a spare key without any rattling.

Care and carrying

To keep the jacket waterproof, wash it twice a year in cool water with a mild soap and rinse it well. Hang it to dry away from direct heat, then tumble it on a low setting for ten minutes to refresh the finish. If water stops beading on the surface, a spray-on reproofer brings it back. The jacket folds into its own pocket and weighs about as much as a small loaf of bread.
