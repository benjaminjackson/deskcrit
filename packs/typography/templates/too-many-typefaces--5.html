Maple Street Library

Quiet rooms, open shelves, and a warm place to read on a wet afternoon. Everyone in town is welcome.

Borrowers may keep up to twelve books for three weeks, and any title can be renewed once by telephone or at the front desk, unless another reader is waiting for it.
