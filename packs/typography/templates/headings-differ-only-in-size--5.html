A year in the life of a village hall

Winter

Whist on Tuesdays, a repair cafe on the second Saturday, and one very old boiler.

Spring

The jumble sale fills the stage by Friday night. By Sunday it is bare again.

Summer

Doors open for the flower show. Judges walk the long tables with clipboards and very straight faces.

Autumn

Harvest supper for a hundred, with every pie baked in a different kitchen.
