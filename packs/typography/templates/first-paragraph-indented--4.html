A weekend loaf

Sourdough

Mix the flour, water, and starter in a large bowl and leave the dough to rest for an hour. The mixture will look rough and sticky at first, which is exactly right.

Fold the dough over itself every thirty minutes for the next two hours, using a wet hand to keep it from sticking.

Cover the bowl with a cloth and leave it somewhere cool overnight.

Shaping

In the morning the dough will have grown and will feel soft and airy. Turn it onto a floured table and shape it gently into a round, tucking the edges underneath.

Place the loaf seam side up in a floured basket and let it rise for another hour.

Heat the oven and the baking pot while you wait.

Baking

Tip the loaf into the hot pot, cut a shallow slash across the top, and put the lid on. Bake for thirty minutes with the lid on, then take it off and bake for fifteen more.

The crust should be deep brown and sound hollow when tapped.

Let the loaf cool on a rack before slicing.
