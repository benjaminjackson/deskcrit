Visiting the swimming pool

Opening hours

The pool is open from seven in the morning until nine at night on weekdays. At weekends it opens at eight and closes at six, and the café stays open an hour longer.

Lane swimming is reserved for the first two hours of each morning, and the rest of the day is open to everyone.

Holiday hours are posted on the board in the foyer a week ahead.

Lockers and towels

Lockers in the changing rooms take a coin that is returned when you open the door again. Please take your belongings with you when you leave, as lockers are emptied every night.

Towels can be hired at the desk for a small charge and returned to the basket by the exit.

Wet shoes are not allowed on the poolside tiles.

Lessons

Lessons for children start at age four and run in six-week blocks, with a short test at the end of each block. Classes are small so that every child gets plenty of attention.

Adult beginners meet on Tuesday evenings in the shallow end.

Places fill quickly, so book at the desk or by telephone.
