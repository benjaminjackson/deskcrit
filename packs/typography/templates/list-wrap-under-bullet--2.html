A plain loaf for Sunday

You will need

  • 500 g strong white flour
  • 350 ml warm water
  • 10 g fine salt
  • 7 g dried yeast

Method

  1. Stir the flour, salt, and yeast together in a large bowl, then pour in the warm water and mix until no dry flour remains.
  2. Cover the bowl with a damp cloth and leave the dough in a warm corner of the kitchen for about an hour, until it has doubled in size.
  3. Tip the dough onto a floured table, fold the edges into the middle, and shape it into a round loaf with a smooth, tight top.
  4. Bake the loaf on a hot tray for thirty minutes, until the crust is deep brown and sounds hollow when you tap the bottom.
