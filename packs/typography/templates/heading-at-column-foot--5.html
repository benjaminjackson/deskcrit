Summer at the lake

The lake is at its best in July, when the water has warmed and the evenings stay light until ten. Families arrive early to claim a spot on the grass, and by noon the shore is dotted with blankets and sunshades.

Rowing boats can be hired from the boathouse by the hour, and life jackets are provided for every passenger.

Swimming and safety

Swimming and safety

Swimmers are asked to stay inside the marked area near the jetty, where the lifeguard keeps watch from a high chair. The deep water beyond the buoys is for boats only.

A kiosk sells ice cream, lemonade, and sandwiches until six, and picnic tables stand in the shade of the willows.

Rubbish bins are emptied each evening.

The midsummer walk

The midsummer walk

On the longest day of the year the town holds a lantern walk around the shore. People gather at the boathouse at dusk, and the path is lit by hundreds of paper lanterns.

The walk takes about an hour at a slow pace and ends with hot chocolate on the jetty.

Everyone is welcome and no ticket is needed.
