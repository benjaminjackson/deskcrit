Landing stage rebuild

The landing stages were rebuilt last spring with wider ramps and a covered waiting area. Benches face the water, and a small kiosk sells tea and bread rolls while the boat is loading.

The ramps now meet the deck at every tide, so prams and wheelchairs roll straight on.
