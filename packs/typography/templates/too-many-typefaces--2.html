The Elm Court Gazette

News from the community garden on the hill

Seed swap

Bring spare packets and seedlings to the table by the gate on the first Sunday of the month. Anything left over goes to the school garden on Monday morning, where the children plant it out in rows.

Please label each tray with the name of the plant and the colour of the flower, if you remember it.

Bees at work

The four white hives behind the willow fence are busy again, and the lavender along the path is humming from morning until dusk. Keep children and dogs on the paths near the hives.

Honey will be shared out in small jars at the autumn fair.
