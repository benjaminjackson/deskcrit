Notes for ferry passengers

The river ferry runs from the north landing to the market quay every forty minutes between early morning and late evening. Each crossing takes about twelve minutes in calm weather, and a little longer when the tide turns against the boat and the crew must hold a wider course around the sandbank.

Bicycles ride on the lower deck at no extra charge. Riders wheel them on after foot passengers have boarded, and lock them to the rail along the side of the deck. The crew asks that bags stay on the bicycle during the crossing, so the deck stays clear for people walking to the stairs.

In winter the timetable thins to one boat each hour, and the last crossing leaves the quay before dusk. Printed timetables are kept in the shelter at each landing, and the noticeboard there lists any changes for the coming week, such as a boat out of service for repairs or a crossing moved for a high tide.
