Pine Ridge

Six kilometres, one steady climb, and a very good view.

Start: the quarry gate

Finish: the lookout

Time: about three hours

Walking guide

Boots and weather

The path is marked with yellow paint on the trunks, but after rain the lower section becomes muddy and slippery, so sturdy boots with a good grip are strongly recommended.

Water and shelter

Water is not available on the ridge, so carry at least a litre for each person. The only shelter is a small timber hut about halfway up, which has a bench and a roof.

Turn back at the hut if the mist comes down.
