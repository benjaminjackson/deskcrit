Saturday bread from the Corner Oven

The Corner Oven bakes its bread in small batches every night and opens the shop door at seven, when the first loaves are still warm from the oven. The sourdough takes two full days to make, because the dough rests slowly in the cold room before it is shaped, and that patience gives the crust its deep colour and the crumb its open, chewy texture.

Cinnamon buns are baked only on Saturdays and usually sell out by ten, so it is wise to order one in advance at the counter or leave a note on the box when you collect your loaf.

The shop has two small tables by the window where you can sit with a coffee and a warm roll, and the staff will happily slice a loaf for you if you ask.
