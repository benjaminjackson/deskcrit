Three days on the coast road

We left the city on a Thursday, with a borrowed car, a paper map, and no plan beyond the first night. The road follows the shore for most of its length, dropping into fishing villages and climbing over headlands where the wind pushes the car across the lane. By noon we had stopped four times, twice for the view and twice for coffee.

The first night was in a small hotel above a harbour, where the owner cooked whatever her brother had caught that day. In the morning the bay was flat and silver, and we walked the cliff path to the next village and back before breakfast. Nobody else was out except a man with two dogs and a heron on the rocks.
