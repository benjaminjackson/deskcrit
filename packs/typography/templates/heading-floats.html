Changing your payment card

You can change the card we charge at any time. The new card is used from your next invoice, and nothing already paid is moved.

Add a new card

Open Settings, choose Billing, and select Add card. Enter the card details and the billing address exactly as your bank holds them.

We place a hold of one pound to check the card, and release it within three working days.

Remove an old card

A card can be removed once another card is set as the default. Select the card, then Remove, and confirm.

If a payment fails

We try again after three days and email the account owner each time. After a third failed try the account moves to read-only until a payment goes through.
