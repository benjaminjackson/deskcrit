Notes for ferry passengers

The river ferry runs from the north landing to the market quay every forty minutes between early morning and late evening.

Crossing times

Each crossing takes about twelve minutes in calm weather, and a little longer when the tide turns against the boat.

Bicycles

Bicycles ride on the lower deck at no extra charge. Riders wheel them on after foot passengers have boarded.

Winter service

In winter the timetable thins to one boat each hour, and the last crossing leaves the quay before dusk.
