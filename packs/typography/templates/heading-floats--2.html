Plots at the Elm Court community garden

Use the barrels first and the tap only when they are empty, ideally early in the morning.

Compost

Kitchen scraps and soft garden waste go into the three bins at the north end of the garden.

Seed swap

Bring a spare packet or a tray of seedlings on the first Sunday of every month.

Beehives

The hives stand behind the fence, so please keep children and dogs on the paths.
