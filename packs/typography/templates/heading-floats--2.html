Leek and potato soup

Serves four · 45 minutes

Ingredients

Three leeks, washed and sliced; four floury potatoes, peeled and diced; one onion; a knob of butter; a litre of vegetable stock; a splash of cream.

Method

Melt the butter in a large pan and soften the leeks and onion over a low heat for ten minutes, without letting them brown.

Add the potatoes and stock, bring to the boil, then simmer for twenty minutes until the potatoes break when pressed.

To serve

Blend until smooth, stir in the cream, and season well. Serve with warm bread and a little chopped chive on top.
