Winter timetable at the harbour

The harbour office opens at seven in the morning from October to March, an hour later than in summer, and the ticket window stays open until the last boat has left. Passengers who hold a season card can board without visiting the window at all.

Boats to the island leave every ninety minutes during the winter, and the first crossing is usually the quietest. In rough weather the captain may skip a sailing, and notices are pinned to the board beside the gate before the first boat is due.

The waiting room has benches, a small stove, and a shelf of paperbacks that anyone may borrow and return on a later trip.

Fares and cards

Crossings are shorter when the wind is from the west, since the boat can run straight along the sheltered side of the bay instead of rounding the point. A normal crossing takes twenty minutes and a rough one closer to thirty-five.

Bicycles travel on the open deck at the stern and should be leaned against the rail. Prams and wheelchairs board by the wide ramp, and the crew will always help with bags.

Dogs on a lead are welcome on every sailing.
