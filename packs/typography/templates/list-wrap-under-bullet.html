Help for ferry passengers

Before you board

  • Check the timetable board at your landing first, because winter sailings run once an hour and the last boat leaves before dusk.
  • Bring exact change for the ticket kiosk if you can, since the machine is slow to give change when the morning queue is long.
  • Wheel bicycles on after the foot passengers have boarded and lock them to the rail along the side of the lower deck.
  • Ask a crew member before you bring a large pram or a heavy trolley down the ramp, so that the gangway is kept clear.

If something goes wrong

  1. Write down the date and time of your crossing and the name of the landing where you boarded the boat.
  2. Describe what went wrong in a few plain sentences, and add the number printed on the back of your ticket.
  3. Leave your note in the box at the quay office or send it to the address on the noticeboard in the shelter.
