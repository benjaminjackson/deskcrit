The Elm Court Gazette

The beans are in

The long bean rows were planted last week along the shed wall, and the first green tips are already pushing through the soil. Gardeners are asked to leave the string lines in place until the plants have found their poles.

Watering is by the barrel only until the hosepipe ban is lifted.1

Swap table

Bring spare seedlings, jars, and flowerpots to the table by the gate on the first Sunday of the month. Anything left over goes to the school garden on Monday morning.

The table is cleared at five, whatever the weather.2

1. The ban applies to all plots. 2. Volunteers wanted to help clear the table.
