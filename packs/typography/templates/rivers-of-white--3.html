Walking the Pine Ridge trail

We go up the hill by the old path, and it is not far to the top. In the sun it is a nice walk, and we all stop to eat at the hut on the way up. If it is wet, the path is wet as well, so we go in boots and we go slow, as it is a bit of a slip on the rocks.

We do not see a tap on the ridge, so we all take a jug of tea or a cup of juice. At the top, we sit on a log by the big gate and eat a bun in the sun, as the sea is far off and we can see a ship or two go by on the blue bay, so we sit up there for an hour or so.

If a mist is on us, we do not go on up. We go back to the hut and sit as we are, and we try to be calm and wait for it to go. We do not go off the path, as it is so easy to go the wrong way in a mist, and a lot of us do it once or twice in a day.

We go as far as the farm gate on a lead, as a ewe and a lamb may be in it, and we go by at a trot, so we do not make a fuss. At the end of it all we are in a good mood, so we go on to the inn for a pie and a pint of ale.
