The Harbour Lights Festival

Unfortunately the extraordinarily popular lantern parade cannot start before nightfall, so photographers and neighbourhood families are encouraged to gather near the fountain, where refreshments, blankets, and accommodation information for out-of-town visitors will be available throughout the evening.

Characteristically, the organisers have arranged straightforward entertainment: accordion musicians, a children's puppet theatre, and approximately forty decorated rowing boats, which will be illuminated individually before the procession reaches the breakwater.
