The Wexley Gazette

Bridge to reopen in spring

The old stone bridge over the Wex will reopen to cars in April, after eighteen months of repairs to its arches. Engineers found that two of the five arches had been weakened by floods, and the council chose to rebuild them with the original stone where it could be saved. Cyclists and people on foot will be able to cross from March.

Market moves indoors

The Saturday market will move into the old corn exchange from next month, ending forty years in the square. Traders said the change was overdue after a wet summer kept many shoppers away. The hall has room for sixty stalls, a small cafe, and a stage for local bands on the first Saturday of each month.

School wins garden prize

Pupils at Mill Lane Primary have won a regional prize for the vegetable garden they built on an unused corner of the playground. The garden now feeds the school kitchen two days a week through the summer term, and the children sell what is left at the gate on Friday afternoons.
