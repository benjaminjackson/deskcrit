Garden club news

The spring plant sale raised more than anyone expected, and the club thanks every member who potted up seedlings in March. The money will pay for a new shed roof.

Seed potatoes are now in the clubhouse, and early varieties can go in the ground as soon as the soil is workable.

Please label every tray clearly with the name of the variety and the date it was sown, so that the next gardener knows what to expect from it.

Slug watch

Slug watch

Slugs have been busy along the north fence this month, so members are asked to check the lettuce beds each evening and to use the wool pellets kept in the red bin.

A rota for watering the communal beds is pinned inside the shed door. Each week needs two volunteers, one for the morning and one for the evening, and sign-up is by initials.

Hoses must be coiled and hung on the hooks after use.

Shed keys

Shed keys

The rota for the shed keys goes round in alphabetical order, starting again each April. If you cannot do your week, swap with a friend and tell the secretary.

Thanks go to the two members who painted the benches by the pond over the weekend. The paint is dry now, and the benches are ready for the first picnics of the season.

Tea and cake are served in the clubhouse after every working morning.
