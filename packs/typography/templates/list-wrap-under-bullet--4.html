The Hillside Parish Newsletter

This month

  • The autumn fair takes place on the green behind the school, with a cake stall, a plant sale, and a table of second-hand books.
  • Volunteers are asked to arrive at eight to set up the tents, and anyone with a trestle table or a spare gazebo is very welcome.

Also this month

  • Hymn books are being repaired in the vestry, so please return any that you borrowed for the summer concerts.

Join in

  • The choir meets in the church hall on Tuesday evenings, and newcomers can simply turn up and stand at the back for the first week.
  • The walking group leaves the bus stop at nine on Saturdays and returns by lunch, with a stop for tea at the farm on the way.

Lost and found

  • A blue umbrella, a child's red scarf, and a pair of reading glasses are waiting in the porch for their owners.
