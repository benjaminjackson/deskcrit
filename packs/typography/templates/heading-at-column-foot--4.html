Setting up your account

Create your account by entering your email address and choosing a password of at least ten characters. A confirmation message arrives within a few minutes, and the link inside it stays valid for one day.

If the message does not arrive, look in the spam folder before asking for a new one. Requesting a second message cancels the first.

Once the address is confirmed you can sign in from any device and your saved lists will follow you.

Choosing a display name

Choosing a display name

Choose a display name that other members will see beside your comments. You can change it at any time from the settings page, and the old name disappears from earlier comments straight away.

Profile pictures are optional. Pictures are cropped to a square, so keep the main subject near the middle of the image.

Notification emails can be turned on or off one topic at a time.
