Using the reading room

Opening times

The reading room opens at nine on weekdays and ten on Saturdays. It closes at five, and an hour earlier on the first day of each month.

Quiet areas

The long tables by the window are for silent reading. Group work is welcome in the side room on the left.

Using the archive

Ask at the desk for the box you need. Pencils only, please, and no more than three boxes at a time.
