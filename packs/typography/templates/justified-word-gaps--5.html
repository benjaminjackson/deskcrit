Walks and waterways

A morning along the canal

Extraordinarily peaceful, comfortably flat, and nowhere near the traffic.

Unfortunately the towpath becomes impassable immediately after heavy rainfall, so photographers, birdwatchers, and neighbourhood joggers should check announcements before disappearing into the countryside with wheelbarrow-sized rucksacks.

Characteristically understated, the lockkeeper's cottage offers refreshments, straightforward directions, and approximately seventeen varieties of homemade marmalade.
