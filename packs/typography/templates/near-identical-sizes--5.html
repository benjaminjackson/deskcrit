Notice board

Harvest supper, Saturday at seven

Tickets are on sale at the post office and cost six pounds, with children under ten free. Please tell us about any food allergies when you buy.

Doors open at half past six. Tables seat eight, and you are welcome to bring a group of friends or sit with neighbours you have not yet met.

The hall will be cleared and swept by ten. Anyone able to stay and help with the washing up will be warmly thanked.
