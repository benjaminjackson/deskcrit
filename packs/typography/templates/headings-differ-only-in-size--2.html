The last ferry of the season

A quiet crossing on the final evening of September.

On the quay

By six the benches were empty. The ticket seller counted coins into a tin and waved to the captain, who had worked the river for thirty years.

On the water

Only nine passengers boarded. A boy with a kite bag stood at the rail while the lights of the north landing came up slowly out of the haze.

At the far bank

The ramp went down for the last time until April. Someone clapped, and then the whole deck joined in.
