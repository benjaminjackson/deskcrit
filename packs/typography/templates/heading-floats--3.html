Walking the Pine Ridge trail

Wear sturdy boots with a good grip, because the lower path is muddy after rain.

Water

There is no water on the ridge, so carry a litre for each person in your group.

The hut

A timber hut halfway up has a bench and a roof, and makes a good place to rest.

Mist

If cloud settles on the summit, stay on the path and turn back at the hut.
