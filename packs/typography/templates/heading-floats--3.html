Terms of hire

Last updated 1 September 2027

1. Booking and deposit

A booking is confirmed when we receive the signed form and a deposit of one quarter of the hire fee. The balance is due fourteen days before the hire date.

2. Cancelling a booking

If you cancel more than thirty days before the hire date we return the deposit in full. After that, the deposit is kept, and within seven days the whole fee is due.

3. Use of the hall

The hall may be used between eight in the morning and eleven at night. Amplified music must stop at half past ten, and the doors to the garden stay closed after dark.

4. Damage

The hirer pays for any damage to the building, the furniture, or the kitchen equipment during the hire.
