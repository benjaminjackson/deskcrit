A slow day on the coast path

Words by the Gazette travel desk. Drawings by the town art club.

The coast path leaves the harbour wall at the end of the fish market and climbs gently through gorse and thrift to the first of three small headlands. Walkers who start early have the cliffs to themselves, and the only sound is the wind in the rail fences.

Most people turn back at the second headland, where a bench looks out over a bay of grey rocks and a single white cottage. Those who go on are rewarded with a tea hut at the lighthouse gate that sells warm scones until four.

The lighthouse from the second headland, late afternoon.
