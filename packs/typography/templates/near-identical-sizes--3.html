Visiting hours

The orchard cafe is open from ten to four, Thursday to Sunday. Dogs are welcome on the lawn. Cakes sell out by two, so come in the morning if you can.

Apple Day at Longfield Orchard

Every October the orchard opens its gates to anyone with a bag and a free afternoon. Forty kinds of apple are laid out for tasting, from sharp green cookers to soft red eaters, each with a hand-written card.

Children can press their own juice, and the old mill stone is turned by a patient donkey at noon.
