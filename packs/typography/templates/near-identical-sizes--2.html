Beekeeping for the curious

A hive is a small town, with its own workers, builders, guards, and a queen who never leaves home. Keeping bees means learning to read that town from the outside.

Begin with one hive in a sheltered corner that faces the morning sun. Put a water dish nearby, with pebbles for the bees to stand on. Check the colony every week in summer, calmly and without smoke unless the day is cold.

Honey is taken only from the top box, and only at the end of the season. Leave the bottom boxes full so the colony can feed itself through winter.
