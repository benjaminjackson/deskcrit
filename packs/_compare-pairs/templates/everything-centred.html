Tell Weekplate who you cook for, what you will not eat, and how long you have on weeknights. It suggests seven dinners that share ingredients, so less food goes to waste by Friday.

Swap any meal you do not fancy and the shopping list changes with it, sorted by the aisles of your usual shop.
