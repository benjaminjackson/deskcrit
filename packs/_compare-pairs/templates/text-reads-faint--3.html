Lowland Rides

The canal path from Brindle Lock to the estuary

By Tom Arden · 6 minute read

The towpath runs flat for thirty-one miles, and for most of that distance it is wide enough for two bikes side by side. Start at the lock keeper's cottage, where there is a tap and a bench, and follow the water east past the old brickworks.

After the third swing bridge the surface turns from tarmac to packed gravel. It is fine on a touring bike with tyres of 32 mm or more, but after heavy rain the stretch by the reed beds holds puddles that cover the whole path.

There are two cafes on the way, at Hatton Wharf and at the sailing club, and both close at four in winter. Bring lights from October on, because the last six miles have no street lamps at all.
