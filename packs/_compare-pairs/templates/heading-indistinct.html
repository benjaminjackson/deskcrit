Change the card you pay with

You can change your card at any time. The new card is charged from your next bill, and nothing is charged when you make the change.

Add a new card

Open Settings, choose Billing, and select Add card. Enter the card number, the expiry date, and the three-digit code from the back, then select Save.

Make it the default

Next to the new card, open the menu and choose Use for payments. The old card stays on file until you remove it.

If the card is declined

Check that the billing address matches the one your bank has. If it still fails, your bank may have blocked the payment, so call the number on the back of the card.
