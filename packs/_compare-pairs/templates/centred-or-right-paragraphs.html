What we do each week

Every Tuesday and Friday our volunteers sort the food that churches, schools, and supermarkets drop at the depot on Carver Road. We pack it into parcels that hold three days of meals for a household, with fresh bread and vegetables whenever local bakers and growers have some to spare.

People reach us through a referral from their doctor, a school, or the housing office, and they can collect a parcel from one of four community centres in the east of the city. If you cannot get to a centre because of illness or caring duties, a driver will bring the parcel to your door.
