Tenant Guide

Before you sign a tenancy

A tenancy is a contract, so read all of it before you sign. Ask the agent to explain any clause you do not follow, and keep a copy of the signed version.

Check the deposit

Your deposit must go into a protection scheme within thirty days, and the landlord must tell you which one. Ask for the certificate if you have not had it.

Take photos on day one

Walk through every room on the day you move in and photograph marks, damp, and broken fittings. Email the photos to the agent so they carry a date.

Know your notice period

Most tenancies need one month of notice from you and two from the landlord. The contract may say otherwise, so check before you plan a move.
