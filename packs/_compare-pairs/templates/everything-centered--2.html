Evening course · 8 weeks

Throwing on the wheel for beginners

Over eight Wednesday evenings you will learn to centre clay, open it, and pull up the walls of a cylinder, then turn those cylinders into mugs and bowls. Each session starts with a short demonstration and then you work at your own wheel.

Clay, glazes, and two firings are included. You take home everything you make once it has been glazed.
