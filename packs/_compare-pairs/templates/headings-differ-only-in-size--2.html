Maps of the inland sea

Forty charts drawn between 1620 and 1790 by the pilots who guided ships through the shoals of the inner firth.

What you will see

Hand-coloured charts, two pilots' notebooks, and a brass sounding lead found in the harbour mud in 1958.

Opening times

Tuesday to Sunday, 10 am to 5 pm, until 2 March. Entry is free, and the gallery is on the ground floor.
