Small-batch ice cream, made on Mill Street

Our milk

Every batch starts with whole milk and cream from one dairy farm eleven miles up the valley. We collect it on Monday and Thursday mornings and churn it the same day.

Our fruit

We buy plums, rhubarb, and berries from growers at the Saturday market and roast or stew them in the back kitchen. When a fruit is out of season, its flavour leaves the board.

Our tubs

Take-home tubs are paper with a plant-based lining. Bring a tub back clean and we take fifty pence off your next scoop, or drop it in the box by the door.

Open Tuesday to Sunday, noon to 9 pm. Closed in January while we clean the machines.
