Leek and potato soup with brown butter

Serves 4 · 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • 3 large leeks
  • 500 g floury potatoes
  • 1 litre vegetable stock
  • 60 g butter
  • A small bunch of chives
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Wash the leeks well, slice them thinly, and soften them in half the butter for ten minutes without letting them brown.
  2. Add the peeled, diced potatoes and the stock. Simmer for twenty minutes until the potatoes break when pressed.
  3. Blend until smooth and season. Brown the rest of the butter in a small pan until it smells of hazelnuts.
  4. Ladle into bowls, spoon over the brown butter, and finish with chopped chives.
