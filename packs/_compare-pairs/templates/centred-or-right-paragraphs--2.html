Arlo digital kitchen scale

A flat glass scale that weighs up to five kilograms in steps of one gram. Press the tare button to zero the bowl, then add each ingredient in turn without doing any sums. The display switches between grams, ounces, and millilitres of water or milk.

It runs for about a year on two AAA batteries and turns itself off after two minutes. The glass top wipes clean, and the feet are rubber so the scale stays put on a wet counter.
