Release notes

Version 4.2, 20 October

Straighten now finds the horizon by itself, and you can still drag the line by hand. Exports to JPEG are about a third faster on older laptops.

Version 4.1, 2 September

Added a soft-proof view for printing on matte paper. Fixed a crash when a folder held more than ten thousand files.

Version 4.0, 15 July

A new editing panel puts light, colour, and detail on one page. Presets from version 3 are converted the first time you open them.
