A restored hay barn for weddings of up to 120 guests

The barn was built in 1870 to store hay for the farm below the ridge. We took it apart beam by beam in 2019 and put it back together with a new oak floor, wide glass doors on the orchard side, and a kitchen that caterers can use from early morning.

Couples have the barn, the orchard lawn, and the cottage for two nights. You may bring your own caterer, band, and florist, and we will walk you through the site as many times as you need before the day.
