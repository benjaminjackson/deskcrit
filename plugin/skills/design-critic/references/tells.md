### Purple-to-blue or violet-to-pink gradients

- **Rule:** Backgrounds, cards, buttons, and the active navigation item use one neutral surface, and an accent color appears only where it carries meaning. A purple-to-blue or violet-to-pink gradient on these surfaces is the stock look of an AI-made template.
- **Look for:** Does a background, card, button, or active navigation item have a purple-to-blue or violet-to-pink gradient fill?
- **Source:** unslop by Damir Sokolovsky, CC BY 4.0, skills/unslop/references/signs.md, commit 620163eedb3529d9cd23a3994ed8d287d06f760d, group "Surfaces and color": "Purple to blue or violet to pink gradients on backgrounds, cards, buttons or the active nav item → one neutral surface, accent only where it means something". Part of unslop by Damir Sokolovsky (@heydamir), CC BY 4.0. Changes: rewritten as a rule and a question.
- **Link:** https://github.com/heydamir/unslop/blob/620163eedb3529d9cd23a3994ed8d287d06f760d/skills/unslop/references/signs.md

### Gradient text in headings, numbers, or the logo

- **Rule:** Headings, numbers, and the logo are set in one solid text color, not filled with a color gradient.
- **Look for:** Is a heading, a number, or the logo filled with a color gradient instead of one solid color?

### Colored glows around elements

- **Rule:** Depth comes from steps between surface colors and from hairlines, not from a colored glow around a card, button, pill, icon, or emoji.
- **Look for:** Does a colored glow or a blurred colored halo surround a card, button, pill, icon, or emoji?
- **Source:** unslop by Damir Sokolovsky, CC BY 4.0, skills/unslop/references/signs.md, commit 620163eedb3529d9cd23a3994ed8d287d06f760d, group "Surfaces and color": "Colored glows, like `shadow-[0_0_20px_rgba(139,92,246,0.5)]`, `drop-shadow` on emoji or glowing pills → remove, depth from surface steps and hairlines". Part of unslop by Damir Sokolovsky (@heydamir), CC BY 4.0. Changes: rewritten as a rule and a question about the visible glow, not the CSS class.

### Color washes and blurred blobs behind content

- **Rule:** The background behind the content and the hero is plain, or it shows one real product visual, with no radial color washes, blurred color blobs, orbs, decorative grids, or glowing lines.
- **Look for:** Is there a radial color wash, a blurred color blob, an orb, a decorative grid, or a glowing line behind the content or the hero?
- **Source:** unslop by Damir Sokolovsky, CC BY 4.0, skills/unslop/references/signs.md, commit 620163eedb3529d9cd23a3994ed8d287d06f760d, group "Surfaces and color": "Radial color washes or blurred blobs behind content → remove", and group "Landing pages and heroes": "Floating blobs, orbs, grids and glowing lines behind the hero → plain background or one real product visual". ### Neon or bright colored borders

- **Rule:** Borders that separate surfaces are hairlines at 6 to 10% opacity, not neon or high-contrast colored lines.
- **Look for:** Does a card, panel, or input have a neon or bright colored border?

### Many near-identical tints of one hue for text

- **Rule:** Text uses three color roles from one neutral ramp, not many near-identical tints of one hue.
- **Look for:** Is the text on the screen set in four or more near-identical tints of one hue, such as several lavenders?

### Every element highlighted

- **Rule:** The accent appears once or twice per screen, on what matters. When every element is highlighted, nothing stands out.
- **Look for:** Is the accent color or a highlight applied to so many elements on the screen that none of them stands out?

### Glass cards with an inner highlight and a colored shadow

### Headings at weight 800 or 900

### Uppercase labels with wide tracking

- **Rule:** Interface labels and other short lines, such as section names, stat titles, and a line above a hero headline, are set in sentence case at normal or slightly open tracking.
- **Look for:** Is a label or other short line, such as a section name, a stat title, or a line above the hero headline, set in uppercase with wide letterspacing?
- **Source:** unslop by Damir Sokolovsky, CC BY 4.0, skills/unslop/references/signs.md, commit 620163eedb3529d9cd23a3994ed8d287d06f760d, group "Type": "Uppercase labels with wide tracking, like `NAVIGATION` or `PAID THIS MONTH` → sentence case at normal or slightly open tracking", and skills/unslop/SKILL.md at the same commit, line 120: "Sentence case everywhere. Labels like `NAVIGATION` or `PAID THIS MONTH` with wide tracking become `Navigation` and `Paid this month`." Part of unslop by Damir Sokolovsky (@heydamir), CC BY 4.0. Changes: rewritten as a rule and a question; the eyebrow example is this catalog's own.
- **Link:** https://github.com/heydamir/unslop/blob/620163eedb3529d9cd23a3994ed8d287d06f760d/skills/unslop/references/signs.md
- **Caveat:** The Typography entry "Letterspace capitals and small caps" asks that capitals be letterspaced when they are used. That entry judges how capitals are set, and this one judges whether a label is in capitals at all. In this catalog's own view, not unslop's, a short kicker above a heading is a print convention in long-form editorial work, so the tell is the stock line above a landing-page hero.

### Numbers in mixed formats

- **Rule:** Numbers of one kind on one screen share one format, with the same decimals, separators, and padding.
- **Look for:** Do numbers of the same kind on one screen appear in mixed formats, such as an amount with cents next to an amount without, or one number padded with a leading zero next to others that are not?
- **Source:** unslop by Damir Sokolovsky, CC BY 4.0, skills/unslop/references/signs.md, commit 620163eedb3529d9cd23a3994ed8d287d06f760d, group "Type": "Numbers in mixed formats, like `$48,210` next to `$124,860.40`, or `07` → one format, tabular figures". ### Icons nudged off the column

### Every element a full pill

- **Rule:** Corner radius follows the size of the element, and a nested element's radius is set from its container's radius. Search fields, buttons, and cards are not all drawn as full pills.
